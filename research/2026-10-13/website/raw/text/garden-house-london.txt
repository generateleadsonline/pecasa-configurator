Bespoke Garden House London – Designed for Work, Fitness, Creativity and Everyday Living
A garden building can create the space you need without changing how the rest of your home works. PECASA designs and installs insulated, made-to-measure garden rooms for homeowners across London, with layouts tailored to the property, the garden and the way the room will actually be used. For homeowners searching for a Garden House London solution, the aim should be more than adding an attractive structure: it should create a comfortable, practical space that performs throughout the year.
Whether you need a dedicated home office, gym, studio, family room, entertainment space or quiet retreat, PECASA plans the building as a complete project. Structure, foundations, glazing, lighting, electrics, internal finishes and climate-control options are considered together so the finished room feels like a purposeful part of your home rather than a seasonal outbuilding.
A Bespoke Garden Room Designed Around Your London Property
London gardens vary enormously. A compact terrace in North London may need a very different design from a larger plot in Wimbledon, Hendon or Ruislip. Access can be narrow, boundaries can be close, privacy can be important and every square metre may need to work hard.
That is why PECASA does not rely on a one-size-fits-all approach. The design can be shaped around available space, intended use, natural light, access, storage, furniture positions and the relationship between the new room and the main house.
A garden building can be planned as:
a quiet garden office for remote or hybrid working;
a home gym, yoga or wellness space;
an art, photography, music or creative studio;
a family room, games room or cinema;
a reading room or private retreat;
a multi-purpose space combining work, storage and leisure.
Built for Year-Round Use, Not Just Summer
A premium garden room should remain useful through changing British weather. PECASA’s published build standard includes Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), ground-screw foundations, high-performance EPDM roofing, modern doors and glazing, integrated electrics and lighting, internal finishing and flooring. AC installation options are also available.
SIP construction combines structural panels with insulation to create a highly insulated building envelope. For homeowners, that means the room can be designed for regular use across the seasons rather than functioning only as a fair-weather summer house.
The specification should be matched to the room’s purpose. Offices may prioritise daylight, data and sockets; gyms may need ventilation and resilient flooring; rooms with a WC or bathroom require early planning for water, drainage, extraction and electrical safety.

Planning Permission and Practical Checks in London
Many domestic outbuildings in England can be constructed under permitted development rights when they meet the relevant criteria. However, permitted development is not automatic for every property or every proposed use.
Factors such as building height, position, total land coverage, listed-building status, designated areas, previous planning conditions and whether permitted development rights have been removed can affect what is allowed. A building intended as separate, self-contained living accommodation also requires different consideration from a typical incidental garden room.
Before work begins, the proposed design should therefore be checked against current planning requirements and the circumstances of the individual property. GOV.UK provides technical guidance on householder permitted development rights, while the relevant London borough remains the authority for site-specific planning matters.
PECASA can discuss these considerations during the design stage, helping homeowners identify where further planning, Building Control or specialist input may be required.

Designed to Fit Your Home and Your Future
A well-planned garden house can adapt as household needs change. Today’s office may later become a study, gym, hobby room or family retreat, so power, lighting, storage and flexibility should be considered from the start.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do I need planning permission for a garden house in London?
Not always. Many garden buildings can fall within permitted development rules, but the limits depend on factors including height, position, land coverage, property type, designated-area restrictions and the intended use. Check the current rules for your property before construction.
Can a PECASA garden room be used all year?
Yes. PECASA designs insulated garden rooms for year-round use and lists SIP construction, modern glazing, EPDM roofing, electrics, lighting and internal finishes within its published premium build approach. Heating or cooling requirements can be discussed during specification.
How long does installation take?
PECASA states that a smaller room can take around 12 working days on site, while a project around 30m² may require roughly 16–18 working days. Bespoke design, ground conditions, site access and the chosen specification can change the programme.
How much does a Garden House London project cost?
The cost depends on size, foundation requirements, glazing, cladding, interior finish, electrical specification, heating or cooling, plumbing and site access. Compare the complete written specification, not only a headline price per square metre.
Can I design the room before requesting a final quotation?
Yes. PECASA provides an online 3D design tool that lets you explore room styles, sizes and specifications and obtain an indicative price before the design is refined around your property.
