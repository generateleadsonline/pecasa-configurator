Create the Ultimate Garden Room with Our Online Tool
At Pecasa we want designing your garden room to be engaging, fun, and easy. Our online tool puts the power in the hands of the homeowner and allows them to control the entire process from start to finish. Our goal is to provide the best experience and we hope to equip you with the best tools to create your home office, gym, studio, or any other living space you want.
Your garden room should be functional, beautiful, and, most importantly, comfortable. Our experience and knowledge of the most crucial elements of your garden room from its layout and size to the overlap of glass and other finishes combined with our insulation will provide you with a garden room that is highly attractive and provides impressive usability.
The Pecasa Garden Room Configurator allows you to customize your garden room in a very interactive way. You will learn the effects and implications of the choices you make in terms of layout, size, and design. You will be able to create a space that meets your needs before we begin manufacturing and installing your garden room. The Garden Room Configurator is the first place you will see transparency in our process, and it will allow you to be free of any guesswork in the beginning.
Some homeowners prefer simplicity and clear structure. Our online garden room planner allows you to clear the bridge of ebbing creativity and flowing ideas. It allows you to structure all your needs and concerns before talking to our design team. This greatly reduces the time spent and increases accuracy as everything is well prepared.
With the garden room builder’s expertise and your flexible option to digitally conceptualize until you are satisfied, Pecasa allows you to experience bespoke design. Our team oversees the entire process once you are satisfied – from off-site manufacture to on-site assembly, and everything in between. This minimizes all disturbances and adheres to your specifications.
The planning and design process prioritizes the homeowners’ needs while providing control, attention to detail, and quality. Garden rooms designed by Pecasa and built using digital tools and human insight are an extension of your home. They are finished to perfection year-round.
Most frequently asked questions
How does the Garden Room Configurator work?
The configurator gives you the freedom and flexibility to design your garden room to your liking. This is done through an easy and straightforward online platform that allows you to visualise the garden room before the consultation.
Once I have used the planner, am I able to change the design?
Of course. Your choices are starting points, and prior to final approval, they can be adjusted in collaboration with our design team.
Is design experience necessary to use the planner?
Certainly not. No prior technical or architectural knowledge is necessary as the tool is designed for the average homeowner.
Is my design going to be exactly how the final build is going to look?
Without a doubt. After you have approved and finalized your design, you can be assured that it will be used for manufacturing and installation exactly as it is.
