Multi-functional Garden Room
Project Overview
This bespoke multi-functional garden room was designed and built by Pecasa Ltd to create a stylish, year-round space combining a home office, family room, and integrated WC.
The client wanted a structure that would feel like a true extension of their home, offering a quiet workspace during the day and a comfortable family space in the evenings.
To achieve a premium architectural look with minimal maintenance, the exterior was finished with Millboard Oak Board & Batten cladding, giving the building a natural timber appearance while benefiting from modern composite durability.
Exterior Design
The exterior design focuses on modern simplicity and natural textures, allowing the building to sit beautifully within the garden landscape.
Key Features
Millboard Oak Board & Batten cladding
Contemporary flat roof design
Integrated LED soffit lighting
Premium anthracite aluminium doors
Modern wall lighting fixtures
Raised step detail around the base
Millboard’s realistic oak finish provides the warmth of timber while being resistant to rot, fading, and weathering, making it ideal for long-term outdoor performance.
Interior Layout
The garden room was carefully designed to include three practical zones, creating a fully functional outdoor living space.
Home Office
A quiet workspace designed for productivity with:
Natural daylight through large glazing
Fully insulated structure for year-round comfort
Power and data points
Space for desk and storage
Perfect for remote working or running a home business.
Family Room
The central area works as a relaxation and entertainment space for the whole family.
Suitable for:
Family movie nights
Reading and relaxing
Kids play area
Guest seating space
The large aluminium doors allow the space to connect seamlessly with the garden.
Integrated Toilet (WC)
To make the garden room fully independent, the structure includes a built-in WC and wash basin.
Features include:
Compact toilet and hand basin
Proper plumbing connection
Ventilation system
Easy-clean surfaces
This allows the garden room to function like a self-contained annex space.
Construction & Performance
As with all Pecasa garden rooms, the building was constructed for durability and year-round comfort in the UK climate.
Key Construction Features
Fully insulated wall and roof system
High-performance glazing
Durable roofing membrane
Professional electrical installation
Long-lasting composite exterior cladding
The insulated structure ensures the garden room remains comfortable in both winter and summer.

The finished project provides a beautiful and highly functional outdoor space that enhances both lifestyle and property value.
The combination of Millboard Oak Board & Batten cladding, large glazing, and integrated lighting creates a garden building that feels both modern and timeless.
