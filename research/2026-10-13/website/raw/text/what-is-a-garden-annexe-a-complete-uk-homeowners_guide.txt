What is a Garden Annexe?
A garden annexe is a self contained living unit on your land. They are also like your average garden shed or summer house but differ as they’re ready for use all year round and include:
• A separate bedroom
• A kitchenette and living room
• A bathroom
• Heating and insulation
• A solid electrical and plumbing system
To sum up, while a garden annexe is still technically separate from your home, it provides an all-in-one living solution.
A more insulated version turns part of the garden into all-year round habitable annexes. With many being finished to provide insulation, double-glazed windows and heating, garden annexes can actually create heated accommodation for year-round living.
