What are Ground Screw Foundations?
Ground screw foundations are relatively modern construction practices, which consist of large steel screws that are drilled deep into the Earth with specialized machinery and become stable anchors for structures built above.
Alongside that, ground screws do not require excavation or curing. They cause less disruption to the surrounding area and work can also be completed in a matter of hours, if not minutes. Due to these advantages, one option that has been adopted and is rapidly being used for modern garden room construction is the ground screw foundations.
