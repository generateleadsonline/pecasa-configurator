What Are Insulated Garden Rooms?
Insulated garden rooms are outdoor structures specifically designed for year-round use, with high-end insulation techniques to keep the internal temperature pleasant. They are not like traditional garden sheds, summerhouses or be-style chalets but rather built to work as authentic extensions of your home.
Similarly, the insulation that is used in the walls or roof or flooring helps to keep it warm during winter and at the same time it prevents excessive heat during summer. This means insulated garden rooms can be used every day, all year round – whatever the weather.
For working or leisure time, garden rooms supply a peaceful and comfy area removed from the residence.
