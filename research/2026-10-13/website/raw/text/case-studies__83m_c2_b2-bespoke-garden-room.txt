83m² Bespoke Garden Room
This impressive 83m² bespoke garden room was designed and built by Pecasa Ltd in Radlett, Hertfordshire, creating a spacious and elegant environment dedicated to entertaining guests and relaxing with family.
The client wanted a large garden building that could function almost like a private guest pavilion, offering comfortable indoor space along with essential facilities such as a kitchen and toilet.
The finished structure blends beautifully with the garden using natural cedar cladding and contemporary architectural lines, creating a warm and inviting space ideal for hosting gatherings throughout the year.
Project Details
Location: Radlett, Hertfordshire
Size: 83m²
Main Purpose: Guest entertainment space
Key Uses
Guest entertaining space
Garden lounge
Dining and social area
Private retreat within the garden
Exterior Design
The exterior was designed to create a modern garden pavilion that feels connected to the surrounding landscape.
Key Features
Natural cedar timber cladding
Contemporary flat roof design
Premium anthracite aluminium doors and windows
Integrated architectural wall lighting
Large patio entertainment area
Clean modern lines
The cedar cladding provides a warm natural appearance while complementing the greenery and mature trees around the garden.
Interior Features
Inside, the building functions almost like a self-contained garden annex, designed to accommodate guests comfortably.
Kitchen Area
A dedicated kitchen space allows food and drinks to be prepared without needing to return to the main house.
Features include:
Worktop preparation space
Storage cabinetry
Power and appliance connections
Bathroom / Toilet
The garden room includes a fully integrated WC to make the building independent from the main property.
Toilet and wash basin
Plumbing connection
Ventilation system
Entertainment Space
The main interior area was designed as an open entertainment zone.
Perfect for:
Hosting friends and family
Garden parties and gatherings
Relaxing lounge area
Indoor-outdoor living
Large glazing brings natural light inside while maintaining views of the garden.
Construction & Performance
As with all Pecasa projects, the building was designed for year-round comfort and durability.
Key construction elements include:
Fully insulated wall and roof system
High-performance glazing
Durable roofing membrane
Professional electrical installation
Long-lasting cedar exterior
This ensures the space remains comfortable throughout all UK seasons.
The Result
This 83m² luxury garden room in Radlett has become the perfect place for the client to host guests and enjoy their outdoor space.
The combination of natural cedar cladding, spacious interior layout, and integrated kitchen and WC creates a garden building that feels both luxurious and practical.
The project demonstrates how a large bespoke garden room by Pecasa can transform a garden into a social and lifestyle space.
