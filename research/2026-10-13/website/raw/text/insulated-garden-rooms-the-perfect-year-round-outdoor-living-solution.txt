Insulated Garden Rooms: The Perfect Year-Round Outdoor Living Solution
Homeowners are smart and seeking out alternatives to costly home extensions whether that was a budget issue or a planning one. Of the many practical, stylish solutions available today, none are quite as chic as an insulated garden room. Insulated garden rooms are created for year around comfort and serve as a multi-purpose room that can be used for work, leisure, exercise, entertainment, or guest accommodation.
Pecasa is a well-known player in the garden building market offering high quality garden rooms, garden offices, annexe or outdoor living spaces tailored to your needs. They focus on strategically engineered insulated garden rooms that integrate functionality, aesthetics with energy efficiency ensuring homeowners maximise the use of space within their outdoor property.
What Are Insulated Garden Rooms?
An insulated garden room is basically a free-standing fully compliant structure situated in the garden or outdoor area of your home, built using high-quality insulation materials in the walls, roof and floor. Insulated garden rooms are built with the intention that they will be used all year around, unlike conventional sheds or summerhouses and are designed to make the room comfortable whatever the outside temperature.
The main benefit insulation offers is temperature control. As in cold months it helps to keep it warm inside and in summer dummy sweating prevents the big heat build-up. This makes for a comfortable and energy-efficient space that you will enjoy all year long.
Reason Insulated Garden Rooms Are Growing In Popularity
The demand for dedicated living and working places has dramatically increased with the shift to remote work, flexible lifestyles, and home-based businesses. Insulated garden rooms are the perfect solution because they give you that separation from the house, but at the same time one which still allows easy access.
