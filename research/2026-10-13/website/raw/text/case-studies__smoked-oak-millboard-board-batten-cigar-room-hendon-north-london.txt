Smoked Oak Millboard Board & Batten Cigar Room – Hendon, North London
Project Overview
This bespoke garden cigar room was designed and built by Pecasa Ltd in Hendon, North London, creating a sophisticated private retreat within the garden.
The client wanted a stylish and comfortable lounge space dedicated to relaxing and entertaining friends, with a design that felt both luxurious and discreet within the garden environment.
To achieve this, the building was finished with Millboard Smoked Oak Board & Batten cladding, giving the structure a deep, rich timber appearance while benefiting from modern composite durability.
Project Details
Location: Hendon, North London
Building Type: Bespoke Garden Lounge / Cigar Room
Cladding: Millboard Smoked Oak Board & Batten
Exterior Design
The exterior design focuses on clean architectural lines and premium materials, creating a modern garden pavilion that feels both elegant and inviting.
Key Features
Millboard Smoked Oak Board & Batten cladding
Contemporary flat roof with LED soffit lighting
Premium corner aluminium glazing system
Architectural wall lighting
Raised base detail for a floating effect
Low-maintenance composite exterior
The Smoked Oak finish provides a refined, darker tone that contrasts beautifully with the aluminium frames and garden surroundings.
Interior Function
The space was designed as a private cigar lounge and relaxation room.
Ideal for:
Hosting friends
Evening drinks and cigars
Relaxing lounge space
Quiet retreat away from the house
The corner glazing opens the room visually to the garden while allowing natural light during the day and a warm ambient atmosphere at night.
Construction Quality
As with all Pecasa garden rooms, the building was constructed for year-round comfort and durability in the UK climate.
Key Build Elements
Fully insulated wall and roof system
High-performance glazing
Durable roofing membrane
Professional electrical installation
Long-lasting Millboard composite cladding
Millboard offers the beauty of natural timber while being resistant to rot, warping, and fading, making it ideal for premium garden buildings.
The Result
This bespoke cigar lounge in Hendon has become a stunning garden feature and a perfect place for the client to unwind and entertain guests.
The combination of Smoked Oak Millboard cladding, architectural lighting, and corner glazing creates a garden room that feels both luxurious and contemporary.
It is a perfect example of how Pecasa transforms outdoor space into elegant lifestyle environments.
