Common Questions:
1. Is planning permission necessary for garden rooms?
Most garden rooms do not need planning permission due to developments. There are guidelines we need to follow based on size and how it is constructed. We provide details for this in the consultation stage.
2. What is the time frame for installation?
Most Pecasa garden rooms are installed in as little as two weeks once manufacturing is complete. The exact timeline depends on the size, specification, and site conditions, but our off-site precision manufacturing allows for significantly faster installation compared to traditional builds.
3. Are Pecasa garden rooms designed for all seasons?
Yes. We have designed these rooms for all year usage, and provide good thermal and acoustic.
4. Is it possible to modify the garden room?
Most definitely. All of our garden rooms are custom, so you are able to modify the layout, width, height, and even the internal features.
