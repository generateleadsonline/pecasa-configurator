Does a Garden Room Add Value to Your Property? What UK Estate Agents Say
Looming in the minds of homeowners in the rapidly changing UK property market are smart renovations and home upgrades that will add value. So, the question is — does a garden room add value to your property?
Garden rooms have shifted from a luxury extension with limited use to an essential element of the home (driven by economic cycle changes, increased flexibility and necessity for living spaces that work harder as people now also work less formally from home) but what does the expert couple got to say?
Pecasa specialise in building designed, fully insulated garden rooms for use all year round. The remainder of this text will demonstrate to those homeowners how the majority of property professionals will assist you in maximising your homes worth.
UK Buyers Have Changed
However, one thing has changed tremendously — buyer expectation and demand over the past few years. So what is it that today’s buyers want? As estate agents to often say, the answer lay below.
• Home offices
• Rooms that serve multiple purposes
• Employment and residence differences
• Fast renovations and upgrades with minimal disruption to the home
Garden rooms deliver exactly that and much more.
A good quality garden room will provide space and value to your property whilst maintaining the structural integrity of your home. Thanks to this, the extra room and flexibility will appeal to many buyers.
Considerations for Property Valuations
Estate agents consider many elements when valuing property:
• Usable square footage
• Construction quality
• Overall condition
• Longevity
• Current market demand
• Potential use
• Spatial demand
For instance, the value of a property is not greatly impacted by the presence of garden structures. On the other hand, insulated, manufactured garden rooms (such as those developed at Pecasa) can offer a true lifestyle living area. Estate agents will often talk of “value-enhancing” as opposed to a decorative space adding value when rooms are completed, and the finish is more than adequate.
Home Improvements- Using a Garden Room as Value-Add Machine
Functional Addition
Features that help a home adapt and respond to the changing needs of a household are arguably the most value-adding, given that buyers prefer well-designed homes. A garden room can be:
• Home office
• Studio space
• Gym
• Retreat
This means that because Pecasa creates a truly individual garden room, the space is designed for real use and will be deemed functional.
Winter insulation
Garden rooms that are not winterised to be enjoyed for the three seasons or classed by estate agents as outbuildings, more than living quarters.
All Pecasa garden rooms are winterised with insulation within the walls, ceiling and floor. Not only does this keep things comfortable year round but that makes the space usable every day. If an estate agent sees a garden room which can be used in the winter as well as the summer, it’s good news for marketability.
Great Remote Worker Opportunity
Purchaser needs develop into a hybrid working mannequin. Houses with a separate insulated garden office trump those making compromises in the house itself.
This is how buyers see a garden office:
• An asset in productivity
• A contribution to mental health
A sustainable solution for the future
This type of added feature tends to find its way into property listings by estate agents because it becomes a selling-point that makes the home stand-out from others.
Will it alter the recorded value of said property?
The value of property is the result of present market conditions, location and demand. A garden room does not add a percentage of value to your property.
However, property professionals do unitedly agree on the following:
• Buyers will bite at quality garden rooms
• Better offer will generate the right demand
• Homes with some unique spaces
• The demand interest will work as positive for the home price.
Garden Rooms vs. Traditional Extensions
One of the most frequently asked questions among homeowners is: does a garden room carry the same value as an extension?
Extensions do add floor space internally but they also bring with them:
• Higher costs
• Structural disruption
• Long construction times
Garden rooms offer a gentler approach. Since they operate as separate structures, they:
• Do not require the existing property to be altered
• Leave existing internal space untouched
• Reduce disruption of the installation
Another example is Pecasa, which builds custom garden rooms, where all the details are intentional for strength and utility; factors that anyone looking to buy will appreciated by estate agents.
Design & Aesthetic Value Impact
Value is not just functional. Equally important is aesthetic value.
A poorly designed garden outbuilding can decrease the value of a property. Conversely, a well-proportioned contemporary garden room can enhance it.
Pecasa garden rooms are created to be part of and add to the property around it.
If estate agents are photographing your property, a premium garden room is probably going to stand out.
Compliance and Buyer Confidence with a Garden Room
Immovable buyers are also suspicious of non-code-built structures. For buyers, we take that stress out of the equation with a garden room that is designed and built to comply with the relevant guidelines meaning it can comfortably live within Permitted Development.
Pecasa designs garden rooms to be compliant within the UK which help reduce client fears of a design not being compliant or a structure not being safe.
Having that peace of mind just encourages better decision-making on the part of buyers.
Market Trends
The future growth of garden room values will be supported by the evolution of the following trends:
• Flexibility in hybrid/remote work
• Homes requiring functional living rooms
• Higher costs of buying bigger houses
• Demand for ready-for-occupancy homes
These trends will be suited to properties with a functional, insulated garden room.
Quality and value direction
A garden room will not add the same value to every property. Here is what will increase a garden room`s worth:
• Full insulation
• Quality Craftsmanship
• Lasting materials
• Practical design
More than a temporary addition, our insulated bespoke garden rooms are of the highest quality and equipped to deliver value over the longer term.
It is a fact that no home improvements guarantee your money back, but solid garden rooms however do.
• Increased market differentiation
• Added value to the property
• Growing interest in the asset
• More aligned with the modern lifestyle they aspire to
• Increased perceived liveable space
Garden rooms by Pecasa give the property significant differentiation and desirability as just as we think in interior design.
Summary
There is an ever-growing demand for function and functionality in the current housing market. Garden rooms provide you with these as a value-adding feature to the home.
A garden room is something made and personalised to last, which gives estate agents an advantage every time. They provide instant lifestyle improvements, and when the time comes, offer ways to lift value inside a homeowner’s real estate portfolio.
Pecasa’s tailored garden rooms give estate agents an advantage in bringing a property to life. Not an off the shelf addition but Pecasa’s bespoke garden rooms deliver a winning edge for any homeowner in the UK’s real estate market.
