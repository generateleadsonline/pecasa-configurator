Ground Screw Site Surveys, Load Testing, Supply & Installation
Pecasa is your one-stop-shop for all ground screw foundation solutions for home, business & specialist construction & civil engineering projects in London and the UK. For clients and contractors, our integrated service provides a streamlined foundation process from the initial site survey and ground assessment right through to load testing, ground screw supply, installation and installation records.
That leads us nicely onto Professional Ground Screw Site Surveys & Testing
The ground conditions between different sites can be quite distinct. The performance and capacity of ground screw foundations can be influenced by factors such as the presence of clay, loose soils, made ground and other ground conditions.
Reporting — Pecasa carries out professional ground screw site surveys and load testing to ascertain if the planned ground screw system is right for your project. This testing can help inform you about actual ground conditions and the performance of the screw before a full install of your foundation.
Our services include:
Ground screw compression testing
Ground screw load testing
Test for pull-out & tensile of ground screws
Installation torque monitoring
Site surveys and ground assessment
Test screw installation
Recorded test results
Ground screw supply and installation
Garden rooms testing, SIP buildings, modular buildings and other structures
Compression Examination & Load Screening for Ground Screw
Pecasa has special testing equipment for your RADIX ground screws and is able to test them in a controlled manner on site. The sample of equipment places an applied load on the test screw, so that its performance and behavior under load can be measured and recorded.
Testing can build up, depending on what the project is asking for:
Loading capacity of a screwed pile
Compression performance
Pull-out or tensile resistance
Installation torque
Proposed ground screw fitment
If the foundation design must be adapted
This can be especially beneficial when engaging with structural engineers, architects, contractors and Building Control.
General Ground Screw Testing (for Building Control (and) Structural Engineers)
More and more garden rooms, garden offices, annexes, SIP buildings, modular structures and other lightweight buildings are being built using ground screw foundations.
For some projects, structural engineer or Building Control may require proof of performance / capacity for the proposed foundation system. Pecasa can assist your architect, structural engineer and Building Control team with the suitable test and installation documentation.
In accordance with the manner of a structural engineer specifying a particular proof load, testing methodology or acceptance requirement, we can review and conduct tests accordingly!

A More Environmentally Friendly Option to Traditional Concrete Foundations
For projects where they can be used, ground screws offer a realistic alternative to traditional concrete foundations. By reducing the amount of concrete and excavation involved, ground screw foundations can minimise disruption to the surrounding site.
Installation can also be significantly quicker than that for traditional foundation types Most importantly, unlike traditional concrete curing periods, the right construction projects can get underway almost immediately following ground screw installation.
This makes ground screws especially appealing for garden rooms, modular structures, SIP buildings &skipped projects where speed, minimizing disruption on-site and greater efficiency of installation play very significant roles.
Ground Screw Installation and Tracks
Once the testing is done and foundation size requirements are determined, Pecasa can provide and install the ground screw system that they need. Installation records can be valuable information for the project team and aid in exercise where relevant communication between installer, contractor, structural engineer and Building Control requirements etc.
By bringing together site assessment, testing and supply & installation we can deliver a much better aligned foundation process with greater visibility for the client from investigation to completion.

You can go over your needs, provide the location of your site and also give project packing as well as structural engineer load requirements. Our team are then able to recommend suitable ground screw testing and installation options for your project.
