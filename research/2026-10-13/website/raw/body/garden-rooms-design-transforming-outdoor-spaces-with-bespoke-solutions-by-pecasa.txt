Why Garden Rooms Design Matters
Garden rooms are so much more than just lonely, empty structures in a garden space. Garden rooms open up new ideas of how architecture and the surrounding landscape can harmonize by designing spaces that are functional, sustainable yet comfortable to live in as an extension of the home.
A garden room professionally designed takes into consideration the following factors:
Available outdoor space
Natural lighting and orientation
Intended use of the room
Energy efficiency
Long-term durability
Visual harmony with the surrounding property
With good planning, the end result will be both practical and pleasant to live in — nice to look at, too — for many years down the line.

Premium Construction for Lasting Performance
Great garden rooms start with great quality build. Pecasa combines top-tier building technologies to deliver unmatched structural performance, energy efficiency and durability.
The garden rooms constructed by them are made of Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs) which insulate well and consume low amounts of energy all year round. Coupled with ground screw foundation systems, these structures can also be built with very little disruption and much quicker than traditional building materials.
Additional premium specifications include:
High-performance EPDM roofing
Fully Tape-and-joint internal finish
Integrated lighting and electrical installations
Quality flooring
