The ANNEX 1 GARDEN ROOM by Pecasa represents the perfect balance of compact design, smart space utilisation, and premium craftsmanship. Whether you require a quiet workspace away from the main house or an elegant additional living area, this garden annex can be tailored to complement your property seamlessly.
Pecasa’s end-to-end service ensures a smooth journey from initial consultation through to design, manufacture, and professional installation. Our experienced team focuses on delivering not just extra space, but a high-quality environment that enhances both daily living and property value.
