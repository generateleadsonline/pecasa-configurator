Pecasa Garden Room Case Studies
At Pecasa, every garden room we create is designed to transform unused outdoor space into a practical, stylish extension of your home. Our Pecasa Garden Room Case Studies showcase how bespoke design, advanced construction methods, and customer-focused delivery combine to produce outstanding results for homeowners across the UK.
One recent project involved a homeowner who needed a dedicated garden office to support long-term remote working. After an in-depth consultation, the Pecasa team designed a fully insulated garden room built with high-performance SIP panels for superior thermal efficiency and structural strength. The completed space now provides a quiet, energy-efficient workspace that can be used comfortably throughout the year. Thanks to our streamlined build process and extensive off-site preparation, installation was completed quickly with minimal disruption to the household.
In another successful project, Pecasa delivered a contemporary multi-purpose garden studio for a growing family. The objective was to create a flexible space that could function as both a guest suite and a relaxation area. Our bespoke approach ensured the new structure complemented the existing property while maximising natural light and interior usability. From design and manufacturing to final installation, our team handled the entire process seamlessly, giving the client complete peace of mind.
What makes Pecasa stand out in the garden room market is our focus on quality, efficiency, and long-term performance. Every garden room is precision-engineered, highly insulated, and built to withstand the UK climate. Our fast installation timelines and end-to-end project management make the journey smooth and stress-free for homeowners.
Through our Pecasa Garden Room Case Studies, you can see how we consistently deliver bespoke garden rooms that enhance lifestyle, increase usable space, and add real value to properties. If you are planning a garden room, Pecasa is ready to bring your vision to life with expert craftsmanship and professional service.
Pecasa is a UK specialist in bespoke garden rooms and garden annexes, serving homeowners across London, Watford, Hertfordshire, and surrounding regions. The company designs, manufactures, and installs fully insulated garden buildings using Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), ground screw foundations, and premium materials as standard
