What Is a Garden Room Planner?
If you are unfamiliar with the concept of a garden room planner, it is basically a design and visualisation tool that enables homeowners to build their outdoor building in relative digital comfort before making a commitment to construction. This provides advance consideration for key aspects such as dimensions, layout, style, glazing and finishes and intended use.
As such, Pecasa has also introduced a Free 3D Design Configurator for customers to simply select their preferred style, set the size and specifications and see how the proposed room looks along with instant design & pricing of their choice. This method is designed to allow for a more collaborative planning phase, while also providing homeowners with clarity over the eventual look of their proposed garden room.
In addition, having the ability to visualize your ideas through a garden room planner also makes it easier to communicate with professional designers. Homeowners are able to conceptualize their idea visually and tailor it for the complexities of their property, lifestyle and practical needs instead of simply using words to describe an idea.
Why Garden Room Planning Matters
Creating a garden room that works successfully requires an awful lot more than picking a pretty exterior. The structure must be fuse well with the handed garden space and function needed for the comfort.
An intentional planning process takes into consideration:
Available garden dimensions
Intended purpose of the room
Positioning and natural light
Door and glazing configurations
Internal layout
Insulation and thermal performance
Electrical requirements
Heating and climate control
Flooring and interior finishes
Long-term flexibility
Tackling these things in advance alleviates uncertainty, and can help create a functional space that continues to work for the owners for years rather than an expensive seldom-used shell.
