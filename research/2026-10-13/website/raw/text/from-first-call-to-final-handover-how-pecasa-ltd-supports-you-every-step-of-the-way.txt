From First Call to Final Handover: How Pecasa Ltd Supports You Every Step of the Way
Choosing a garden room isn’t just about the building — it’s about trusting the people behind it.
At Pecasa Ltd, we believe the best garden rooms are created through a clear, supportive process where the customer feels informed, involved and confident from day one. That’s why we stay with our clients from the very first conversation to the final handover — and beyond.
Step 1: The First Conversation – Listening Comes First
Every Pecasa project starts with a simple conversation. We take the time to understand:
How you plan to use the garden room
Your garden space and layout
Your style, budget and expectations
There’s no pressure, no rushed decisions — just honest advice.
“From the first call, they really listened to what we wanted. We never felt pushed into anything and the advice was genuinely helpful.”
Step 2: Design That’s Truly Bespoke
Once we understand your needs, we move into the design stage. This is where your ideas start becoming a real, practical plan.
We guide you through:
Size and layout options
External finishes and cladding
Doors, glazing and internal setup
Heating, electrics and lighting
Everything is explained clearly, so you always know what you’re getting.
“The design process was smooth and well explained. We felt involved the whole way and the final design was exactly what we wanted.”
Step 3: Clear Pricing & Honest Communication
One thing our customers often mention is clarity. We believe transparency builds trust, so we provide clear quotes and realistic timelines.
No hidden surprises. No confusing technical jargon.
“Pricing was clear from the start and communication was excellent throughout. Everything happened exactly as promised.”
Step 4: Manufacture & Preparation
Your garden room isn’t just built on-site — much of the work is carefully prepared in advance. This means:
Better quality control
Faster installation
Less disruption to your home and garden
Our team ensures everything is ready before we arrive, so installation runs smoothly.
“You can tell they know exactly what they’re doing. Everything was organised and the build process was incredibly efficient.”
Step 5: Professional Installation
Installation day is where everything comes together. Our experienced team works cleanly, efficiently and respectfully in your space.
Most customers are surprised by how quick and tidy the process is.
“The team were polite, professional and left everything spotless. The build quality is outstanding.”
Step 6: Final Handover & Aftercare
We don’t disappear once the build is finished. Before handover, we:
Walk you through the space
Explain electrics, heating and features
Make sure everything is exactly right
And if you ever have questions later, we’re only a phone call away.
“Even after completion, Pecasa were happy to help with any questions. Fantastic service from start to finish.”
Why Customers Recommend Pecasa Ltd
Our Google reviews often mention the same key points:
Friendly, knowledgeable team
Excellent communication
Premium build quality
Stress-free experience
“We would recommend Pecasa without hesitation. From start to finish the service and quality were exceptional.”
A Local Company You Can Trust
Pecasa Ltd proudly serves North London, Watford and Hertfordshire, offering premium bespoke garden rooms built with care, precision and pride.
We don’t just build garden rooms — we build long-term relationships with our customers.
