So here are the most common use cases:
Home offices for remote working
Personal gyms and fitness studios
Music and art studios
Entertainment rooms
Children’s playrooms
Yoga and meditation spaces
Guest accommodation
Family home turned into two introverted open family living spaces.
These nature-inspired spaces offer a convenient way for homeowners to enjoy complete flexibility while remaining tucked away from the main house.
