TGO3 GARDEN ROOM
SHARPLY MODERN
The TGO3 design is bold and modern with its three quarter surrounding sqaured hood, edge and decking. This enclosed feature is highlighted by the use of graphite or black accents.
The TGO3 GARDEN ROOM by Pecasa is a modern and highly versatile outdoor building designed for homeowners who want to extend their living space with style and efficiency. Blending contemporary architecture with precision construction, this bespoke garden room offers a practical solution for work, relaxation, or multi-functional family use.
At Pecasa, every garden room is created to integrate seamlessly with your home and garden. The TGO3 model reflects this design philosophy through its clean lines, smart proportions, and premium finish. Whether you need a dedicated home office, creative studio, home gym, or quiet retreat, this garden room can be fully customised to match your lifestyle and spatial requirements.
Pecasa specialises in designing and building bespoke garden rooms that enhance both usability and property value. Their structures are fully insulated and engineered for year-round comfort, allowing the space to function like a natural extension of the home. Built with high-quality materials and advanced construction techniques, the TGO3 is designed for durability in the UK climate while maintaining excellent thermal performance.
One of the key advantages of choosing Pecasa is the streamlined build process. Much of the structural work is completed off-site in controlled workshop conditions, helping to reduce installation time and minimise disruption to homeowners. This efficient approach ensures a faster, cleaner on-site experience without compromising build quality.
The TGO3 GARDEN ROOM is also highly adaptable. Clients can personalise glazing options, exterior finishes, internal layouts, and electrical features to create a space that truly reflects their needs. This flexibility makes it suitable for a wide range of residential applications, from productive workspaces to comfortable lifestyle rooms.
If you are looking to transform your garden into a stylish, functional, and future-ready space, Pecasa’s TGO3 model offers a smart investment that combines bespoke design, premium materials, and long-term performance.
