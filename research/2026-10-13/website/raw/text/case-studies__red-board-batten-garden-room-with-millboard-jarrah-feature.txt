Red Board & Batten Garden Room with Millboard Jarrah Feature
Family Garden Room – Pinner, North West London
This bespoke family garden room was designed and built by Pecasa Ltd in Pinner, North West London, creating a beautiful and practical space for relaxing, entertaining guests, and spending time with family.
The design combines red Board & Batten cladding with a Millboard Jarrah feature, forming a distinctive triangular architectural detail that gives the building a modern and unique character.
Positioned within a mature garden surrounded by trees, the garden room blends naturally into its environment while still making a bold architectural statement.
Exterior Design
The exterior focuses on clean lines and strong materials, creating a contemporary garden building with a warm natural feel.
Key exterior features include:
Red Board & Batten cladding
Feature Millboard Jarrah triangular design
Contemporary flat roof profile
Premium anthracite aluminium sliding doors
Modern exterior wall lighting
Raised entrance step
The combination of the deep red cladding and the Jarrah Millboard accent colour creates contrast and visual interest while maintaining a natural timber appearance.
Interior Space
Inside, the building was designed as a multi-purpose family room, providing a quiet space separate from the main house.
The room is perfect for:
Family relaxation
Children’s play area
Movie and entertainment space
Hosting friends and guests
Large glazed doors bring natural light into the room and create a strong connection with the garden.
Construction & Performance
Like all Pecasa garden rooms, the building was constructed for year-round use in the UK climate.
Key construction elements include:
Fully insulated wall and roof system
High-performance double glazing
Durable flat roof membrane
Professional electrical installation
Long-lasting composite cladding materials
This ensures the space remains comfortable throughout all seasons.
The Result
This red Board & Batten garden room in Pinner has transformed the garden into a warm and welcoming family space.
The combination of Millboard Jarrah detailing, modern glazing, and contemporary design creates a garden building that is both practical and visually striking.
The project demonstrates how a bespoke Pecasa garden room can extend living space while enhancing the overall garden design.
