Client Brief
The homeowner wanted a warm, cosy place in the garden where she could relax and unwind. A WC was important so the room could function independently without frequent trips back to the house.
Challenge
The project needed to feel like a genuine retreat rather than a generic garden office. Comfort, privacy and a welcoming finish were more important than creating a formal workspace.
Pecasa Solution
Pecasa delivered a 25 m² garden room on 30 ground screws, finished externally in Millboard Burnt Oak. Large opening doors connect the room to the garden, while the completed interior includes a WC, air conditioning and a feature wall to support comfortable everyday use.

Result
The finished room gives the client a comfortable, self-contained place to switch off close to home. Its warm external finish and practical facilities support the cosy, informal character at the heart of the brief.
