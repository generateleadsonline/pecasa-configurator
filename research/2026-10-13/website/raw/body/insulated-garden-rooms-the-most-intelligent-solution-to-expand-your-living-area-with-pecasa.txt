Pecasa, based in Brighton, is the UKs specialist for the design, manufacture and build of high quality insulated garden rooms that utilise cutting-edge construction technology with unique handcrafted techniques. Every project is uniquely designed to suit the lifestyle of each homeowner and to provide optimal durability, energy efficiency, and contemporary styling.
Why Choose Insulated Garden Rooms?
Unlike temporary garden sheds or seasonal structures, insulated garden rooms are designed for regular use in year-round. Good insulation holds the air in place so that high outdoor temperatures cannot easily penetrate through to your home during summer, or vice versa in winter giving you a space that feels as if it were a natural extension of your indoor living space.
If you have a home business, work from home, have creative hobbies or just want more space for family without the hassle of putting an extension on the house—now you can enjoy comfort and convenience in your own insulated garden room. Less cooling, and heating is another plus point, which occurs better thermal efficiency that will provide benefits even in the long run.
Construction designed for all four seasons premium
Pecasa builds every insulated garden room with the energetically-efficient contemporary building method known as Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs). SIPs have great insulation values as well as structural strength and durability, so the home is comfortable no matter the season.
