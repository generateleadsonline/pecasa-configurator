Why Pecasa’s Free Survey and Free 3D Design Make All the Difference
Investing in a garden room is a big decision. Homeowners want confidence — not guesswork. At Pecasa Ltd, we believe clarity should come before commitment, which is why we offer a free site survey and free 3D design as part of our service.
This approach allows our customers to make informed decisions, avoid surprises, and feel confident from day one.
A Free Survey: Getting It Right From the Start
Every garden is different. Ground levels, access, layout, drainage and boundaries all play a crucial role in how a garden room should be designed and built.
Our free on-site survey allows us to:
Assess your garden properly
Identify any challenges early
Advise on the best position and size
Ensure compliance with permitted development rules
By visiting your property before anything is agreed, we eliminate assumptions and reduce the risk of unexpected issues later.
“The survey was very thorough. They checked everything and explained what was possible before we made any decisions.”
Honest Advice, Not Sales Pressure
Because our survey is free, homeowners can explore options without feeling locked into a decision. We offer honest guidance on what will work best — even if that means suggesting alternatives.
This builds trust and helps customers feel supported rather than sold to.
“There was no pressure at all. They explained everything clearly and gave genuine advice.”
Free 3D Design: See Your Garden Room Before It’s Built
One of the biggest advantages Pecasa offers is free 3D visualisation. This allows you to see exactly how your garden room will look in your space — before construction begins.
With a 3D design, you can:
Visualise the size and proportions
See how it fits into your garden
Explore different finishes and layouts
Make changes with confidence
This removes uncertainty and helps ensure the final result matches your expectations.
“The 3D design really helped us picture everything. We made a few changes and the final build was exactly what we expected.”
Fewer Changes During the Build
Because decisions are made early using accurate surveys and 3D visuals, there’s less need for changes during construction. This means:
Smoother installation
Better time management
Less stress for homeowners
Planning properly from the start leads to better results at the end.
Designed Around Your Lifestyle
The free 3D design process isn’t just about looks — it’s about function. We design around how you plan to use the space, whether it’s:
A home office
A gym or studio
A treatment or therapy room
A multi-use family space
This ensures the layout, lighting and features work perfectly for your day-to-day needs.
Transparency Builds Confidence
By offering both the survey and design free of charge, Pecasa shows confidence in its process and product. We believe customers should understand exactly what they’re getting before committing.
“It was reassuring to see everything designed in advance. No surprises — just a clear, professional service.”
Why Pecasa Does It Differently
Many companies charge for surveys or design work. Pecasa chooses not to, because:
It builds trust
It encourages better planning
It leads to happier customers
Our goal is to create garden rooms that people love — and that starts with clarity, not commitment.

Free site survey

No pressure, no obligation
