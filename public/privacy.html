Your design. Your privacy.

This independent Pecasa configurator concept is built and hosted by Generate Leads Online. It helps you explore a design and save a project enquiry. It is not an order, binding quotation or a replacement for Pecasa’s technical review.

What is saved

Your room choices are saved on your device so you can return to them. When you submit the form, your name, email, postcode, any optional phone or project information, marketing preference, design and preview image are saved securely in a private Netlify data store. Your estimate and PDF are generated from that accepted design.

How your enquiry is used

The information records the project request and provides your personalised estimate information and design pack. Access is limited to the site operator and authorised project administrators. No CRM or automatic email delivery is connected to this concept. Download your PDF and contact Pecasa directly to discuss the project; the contact buttons open your phone or email app.

Storage and sharing

Accepted designs expire after 90 days in the application. Stored records require a separate deletion process by the site operator; expiry is not an automatic deletion promise. Netlify provides the hosting and private storage. Product-only share links contain your design choices and exclude your contact details. Your private PDF link is an access link: keep it private. Your browser session stores that private access token so you can return to your accepted estimate in the same tab.

Your choices

Optional marketing consent is separate from saving the enquiry, is unchecked by default, and does not trigger an automated email subscription. To ask about your data, request deletion or withdraw an optional preference, contact Generate Leads Online through your existing project contact and include your design reference. You can clear your local draft using your browser’s site-data settings.

Analytics

The studio emits product interaction events to an on-page data layer to support future conversion measurement. This concept has no third-party analytics tag connected. Events contain design stage and product choices, not your email, postcode, phone or notes.

Pecasa’s own website has its own privacy notice. This notice applies to the independently hosted design studio.
