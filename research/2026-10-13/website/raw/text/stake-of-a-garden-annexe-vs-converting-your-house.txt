Both options provide you with the opportunity to obtain additional living space without placing your home on the market. Nevertheless, when it comes to the original expense, extended-term value proposition, flexibility and functionality factors- the answer varies primarily with several relevant variables.
Here at Pecasa, a London and surrounding area based garden room specialist we focus on building insulated garden rooms and annexes that give you modern day living space to enable year round use. Getting to Know the Options
Converting Your Existing House
House conversions typically consist of:
• Loft conversions

• Internal structural changes
• Rear extensions
All of these options will need you to make more extensive internal or external alterations to the main house and may cause havoc with your day-to-day life throughout the building work.

An annexe in the garden is an independent space, built entirely within the confines of a garden. The annexe can be anything from: Custom built by professional garden room builders.
