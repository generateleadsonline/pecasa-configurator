A garden room company that designs, manufactures and installs

Pecasa Ltd is a garden room company based in Watford, Hertfordshire. Since 2017 we have completed more than 120 bespoke garden rooms, garden offices and garden annexes. We manufacture our own Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs) in Watford, and our core team manages every build. Our team brings more than 100 years of combined construction experience.

Every building is designed from scratch around your garden, your home and the way you will use the space. We do not sell flat-pack kits or off-the-shelf models. Buildings typically range from 4 m² to 90 m², and our most popular size is around 30 m².

Why homeowners choose Pecasa

Truly bespoke garden room design

Size, layout, roof form, glazing and finishes are designed for your garden and planning position.

SIP panels made in our own factory

Walls, floor and roof are built from SIPs. EPS70 insulation is standard, available from 75 mm to 250 mm, and PIR insulation is available as an upgrade, for genuine year-round comfort.

British-made Radix ground screw foundations

Radix ground screws are our standard foundation system, subject to ground conditions and site suitability.

A finished room, not a shell

Plasterboard walls with a tape-and-joint finish, 12 mm premium laminate flooring, lighting, electrics and Cortizo aluminium doors and windows come as standard.

Clear, VAT-inclusive prices

Prices start from £23,000 including VAT for a 3 m × 2.8 m garden room. Every Pecasa building is bespoke, and your final quotation reflects the design, specification and site requirements. You will also receive a free 3D design and a written, VAT-inclusive quotation, normally within seven days of your survey. Quotations remain valid for three months.

Long-term protection

A 10-year IWA insurance-backed warranty on qualifying projects, subject to registration and policy terms. We also usually offer a free aftercare visit around six months after handover.

    How long does a Pecasa garden room installation take?

    Do garden rooms need planning permission?

    In England, many garden rooms used for purposes incidental to the house, such as an office, gym or studio, fall within permitted development and do not need planning permission, provided they meet the relevant size, height and location limits. For example, a building within 2 m of a boundary must be no more than 2.5 m high under England’s guidance. Planning rules differ elsewhere in the UK. Flats, listed buildings, conservation areas and self-contained annexes have different rules. We give guidance at your free survey and can recommend an external planning consultant where an application is needed.

    Are Pecasa garden rooms suitable for year-round use?

    Yes. SIP walls, floor and roof, Cortizo aluminium glazing and an EPDM roof create a well-insulated building. Air conditioning or heating can be added and is specified in your quotation.

    What is included as standard?

Site clearance, electrical supply connections to the building, water and drainage connections, skips and parking permits are priced separately.
