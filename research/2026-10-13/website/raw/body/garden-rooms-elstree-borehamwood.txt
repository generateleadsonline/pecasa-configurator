Navigating the Planning Permission Process
While many garden rooms fall under permitted development rights, it’s always wise to double-check with local authorities to ensure compliance. Some considerations include:
Overall Height: A garden structure near the property’s boundary should not exceed certain height limits.
Purpose of the Building: If the room is for ancillary use only (e.g., home office, gym, summer house), it will likely be covered under permitted development. Using it as a separate living accommodation might require additional permissions.
Conservation Areas: If you live in or near a conservation area, you might face stricter guidelines regarding the design and position of your garden room.
Check the Planning Portal: The UK Government’s Planning Portal website offers detailed guidance. However, consult our experts at Pecasa or contact the local planning department directly for peace of mind.
