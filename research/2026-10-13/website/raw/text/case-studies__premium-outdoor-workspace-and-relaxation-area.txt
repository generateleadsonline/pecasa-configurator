Premium outdoor workspace and relaxation area
Project Overview
This bespoke garden room was designed and built by Pecasa Ltd to create a modern, functional space that blends seamlessly with the garden while providing a comfortable year-round environment.
The client wanted a premium outdoor workspace and relaxation area that felt connected to the garden but offered the comfort and insulation of a modern building.
The result is a sleek cedar-clad garden studio with a covered decking area, perfect for working, entertaining, or simply enjoying the garden in all seasons.
Key Features
Size: Approx. 7m x 4m garden room
Use: Home office & relaxation space
Location: UK residential garden
Exterior
Natural vertical cedar cladding
Modern flat roof design
Integrated LED soffit lighting
Large covered veranda / canopy
Premium black aluminium sliding doors
Raised composite decking platform
Contemporary black detailing
Interior
Fully insulated year-round garden room
Double glazing for thermal efficiency
Electrical installation with LED lighting
Smooth plastered and decorated walls
Premium flooring finish
Design Approach
The design focuses on clean architectural lines and natural materials.
The warm tones of the cedar cladding contrast beautifully with the black aluminium frames and decking, giving the structure a modern yet timeless appearance.
The covered decking area extends the usable space outdoors, allowing the client to enjoy the garden even during the UK’s unpredictable weather.
Build Quality
Like all Pecasa garden rooms, this structure was built using high-performance materials to ensure durability and comfort:
High-performance insulated wall system
Professional electrical installation
Weather-resistant exterior finishes
Long-lasting roofing system
Precision craftsmanship throughout
This ensures the garden room remains comfortable in both winter and summer, making it a true extension of the home.
The Result
The finished space provides a quiet, stylish retreat just steps from the house.
The combination of natural cedar, modern lighting, and the spacious covered deck creates a versatile environment that works equally well as:
Home office
Garden lounge
Creative studio
Entertaining space
