Client Brief
The homeowner wanted a substantial garden room with a WC and an additional small room, but she did not want a conventional rectangular building to consume more garden than necessary.
Challenge
The plot’s angled boundaries made a standard rectangular room inefficient. The design needed to maximise usable internal area while protecting valuable outdoor space.
Pecasa Solution
Pecasa developed a bespoke tapered, triangular footprint that follows the garden boundaries. The resulting double-angled form accommodates the main room, a WC and a smaller side room. Red-toned Millboard cladding gives the architecture a distinctive finish, and air conditioning supports year-round comfort.

Result
The client gained more useful internal space without surrendering unnecessary garden area. Rather than asking the plot to accommodate a standard box, Pecasa shaped the building around the site and the way the owner wanted to use it.
