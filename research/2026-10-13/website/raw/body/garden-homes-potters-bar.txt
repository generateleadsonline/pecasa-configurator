Garden Studios vs. House Extensions: What’s the Difference? 🏗️🤔
Should you build a Garden Studio or opt for a full-fledged house extension? Let’s compare these two popular choices:
1. Complexity of the Build
House Extension: Typically involves integrating additional rooms into your main home structure, often requiring extensive foundation work, planning permission, and disruptions to your existing living space. Construction can be noisy, messy, and lengthy. 🔨🚧
Garden Studio: A Garden Studios build is often simpler and swifter. Often, these are stand-alone structures in your garden, minimizing disruption to your day-to-day life. You can carry on with your routine while we get to work outside. 🌳👌
2. Planning Permission
House Extension: You’re more likely to need planning permission, especially if altering the structural layout or local property lines. Regulations can be stricter, adding to the time and paperwork involved. 📝📋
Garden Studio: Many garden buildings can be constructed under Permitted Development rights, meaning you may not need full planning permission—though this depends on specific height, size, and usage rules. Of course, Pecasa will guide you through these steps, ensuring compliance with local regulations in Potters Bar. ✅🔍
3. Cost
House Extension: Generally, extensions are more expensive because they integrate fully into your existing property, requiring substantial materials, professional labor, and potential reconfiguration of plumbing or wiring. 💸💰
Garden Studio: Due to less complicated foundations and smaller scale, Garden Rooms often cost less than a house extension. You’ll also typically see a faster return on investment, especially if you want to add a home office or a studio that immediately supports your day-to-day activities. 💡🏷️
4. Flexibility and Versatility
House Extension: Once you commit to a specific design, you’re mostly locked into that layout. Changing it later can be challenging (and pricey). 🔒
Garden Studio: You can customize the interior and exterior design to suit your needs. Plus, you can easily repurpose the space over the years—turn it into a teen hangout, a man cave, or a Summer House for entertaining guests. 🔄🌟
Given these factors, it’s no surprise that many Potters Bar homeowners opt for a Garden Room Design.
