Garden Rooms North London: Enhance Your Home with Bespoke Outdoor Living by Pecasa
With many homeowners looking for practical options to gain a bigger living area (and perhaps create an office space along the way), garden rooms in North London have become one of the hottest home improvement trends today. They offer more functional area at the fraction of the price, hassle and duration of a conventional home extension. Featuring comfort, usefulness and worth on the long term, a professionally designed garden room will provide you everything you have to work in peacefully from home or spend your spare time exercising, creating or relaxing.
Pecasa is an experienced specialist in bespoke garden room design and construction of high-quality build, innovative structures with a modern aesthetic. As a unit, their bespoke solutions allow homeowners to turn unused outside space into classy and useful areas that can be used all-year-round.
The Benefits of Garden Rooms for Your Home in North London
The exorbitant price of properties combined with scarcity of land space within our homes has caused the average North London homeowners to explore better prospects of making good use of their available property. Garden rooms offer a way to have additional space that is still separate from the main house but having all the comforts of an indoor living space.
Modern-day garden rooms tend to be fully insulated, double-glazed and constructed from high quality materials — unlike the typical British garden shed with flimsy walls. These are designed to be used throughout the year that can be utilized as a workspace, a lounge for relaxation, fitness or a hobby room and even a place to have guests over.
Having a garden room designed by professionals will not only make your garden look even more attractive, it can also greatly enhance your space and add value to the property as well.

Premium Construction for Lasting Quality
Construction quality is a key aspect that contributes to the durability and comfort of a garden room. Pecasa also implements advanced Structural Insulated Panel (SIP) technology, which provides great thermal performance, energy efficiency and structural integrity.
Their garden rooms are engineered to retain warmth during the winter months and remain crisp in the summer and fit for everyday use, whatever time of year.
