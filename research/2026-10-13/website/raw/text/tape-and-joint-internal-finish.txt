Tape-and-joint internal finish
A high-quality garden room should feel like a natural extension of your home — not a basic outbuilding. At Pecasa, every bespoke garden room and annexe is completed with Tape-and-joint internal finish to deliver a smooth, modern, and ready-to-use living space.
Unlike many standard garden buildings that use visible panel joints or basic wall linings, our Tape-and-joint internal finish create a clean, seamless appearance similar to a traditional home extension. This premium finish not only enhances the visual appeal of the space but also provides a durable and practical surface designed for long-term use.
One of the key benefits of a tape-and-joint internal finish is its versatility. Once completed, the walls are ready for painting, decorating, shelving, or wall-mounted features, giving homeowners complete freedom to personalise the space. Whether you plan to use your garden room as a home office, studio, gym, or guest area, the professional Tape-and-joint internal finish provides a clean, refined foundation for your chosen interior style.
Our skilled installation process ensures every interior surface is carefully prepared and finished to a high standard. When combined with our SIPs construction, high-performance glazing, and integrated electrics, Tape-and-joint internal finish help create a garden building that feels comfortable, polished, and truly residential in quality.
At Pecasa, we include premium interior finishing as part of our commitment to delivering garden rooms and annexes that exceed typical market expectations.
Frequently Asked Questions
What are Tape-and-joint internal finish?
A Tape-and-joint internal finish involves sealing and finishing the gaps, corners, and screw heads between boards to create a smooth, seamless surface. The finished walls provide a clean, professional appearance and are ready for painting, wallpapering, or other decorative finishes.
Why does Pecasa include Tape-and-joint internal finish?
Pecasa includes professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish to ensure every garden room feels like a true extension of the home, providing a clean, seamless, and durable finish compared with basic panel systems.
Can I paint or decorate the Tape-and-joint internal finish walls?
Yes. Once the Tape-and-joint internal finish is complete and dry, the walls are ready for painting, decorating, shelving, and other interior customisation.
Do Tape-and-joint internal finish improve the overall look of the garden room?
Absolutely. A professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish creates a clean, modern appearance that enhances the overall feel and perceived quality of the garden room.
