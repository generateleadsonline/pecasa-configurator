Garden Room Configurator: Build Your Outdoor Vision with Pecasa
Garden rooms are becoming more popular among homeowners as more people choose to spend more time outdoors. A garden room gives you many possibilities as you can use it as a workspace, gym, or relaxation area. To assist you in this, we offer a Garden Room Configurator. This is a very valuable asset as it allows you to customize and plan the construction of your garden room and how it will complement your garden.
At Pecasa, we offer advanced technology that will aid in customizing and selecting the various options and making simple the task of outlining the space to your specifications.
What is a Garden Room Configurator?
With a Garden Room Configurator, you have the ability to customize your design in terms of dimensions, layouts, and a variety of other elements. Rather than having to work with a design that has been used many times before you, this allows for far greater customization.
Pecasa’s configurator allows homeowners to:
– Design their own dimensions
– Design their own layout
– Design their own look
– Design their own style to match them and their home
From this, all of your garden rooms will also look good with your home and outside garden You may be asking, why is a garden room configurator important to use? Because You’ll be faced with so many choices when thinking about a garden room, a configurator makes a lot of these choices easier.
1. You Have Full Control Over The Design
Pecasa’s garden room configurator allows you to not use any of the templates. You will have the choice to make your room as large as you like. You may also create a workspace or multi-function rooms.
2. You Will Have The Choice of the Design and Look Rooms
The main benefit is uncertainty will not exist. Your end design will be the look you want.
3. The Process of Planning Will Replace the Designs
Instead of doing revisions and going back and forth, the configurator will allow you to design without the hassle. This will save you all the time and effort of doing these because everything will be done quickly.
4. Outdoor Living Spaces Made Just for You
Each homeowner has their individual preferences. Whether it be a garden office, creative studio, or private retreat, the configurator helps create spaces tailored to your lifestyle.

Pecasa Design System
Each well-thought-out garden room should fit well with the main house. Pecasa’s design system helps create a garden room that integrates well with the house and the other external areas of the property.
Garden Room Customization
Pecasa’s configurator offers the best streamlined design experience.
1. Custom Size

2. Layout Design
The configurator lets you set the internal layout according to your specific needs, be it work, leisure or storage.

Select finishes and styles that best fit your preferences, and that reflect the look of your current property.

7. Ease of Use

8. Emphasis on Flexibility
Pecasa has a focus on design/flexibility with the aim of ensuring your garden room is tailored to your needs.
9. Complete Design Guarantee

How to Use the Garden Room Configurator
Using Pecasa’s Garden Room Configurator is simple and quick. This is how it works:
Begin with the Essentials
Start with the garden room’s intended use. Will it be an office, a gym, or a leisure space?
Choose the Dimensions and Internal
Make your selection on the internal layout and dimensions.
Design Preferences
Choose your preferred elements that fit your home and design the exterior features of your choice.
Look at the Garden Room
Save and design the garden room the way you like it.
Design the Garden Room
Your design is set. Proceed to the additional steps and get your garden room constructed.
The Beginning of the Garden Room Design
The use of the garden room configurator is the first step to show the freedom that the homeowner’s have. The more simple and less stressful the design decisions are for the owner, the more confident they become.

The Garden Room Configurator provides a user-friendly platform for designing bespoke outdoor spaces. Pecasa provides better opportunities for end-users by letting them design their own spaces instead of settling for pre-existing options.

Pecasa’s configurator gives the end-user a more efficient and effective way to design their garden room.
