Home Office Garden Room with Skylight – Wimbledon, London
This bespoke Pecasa garden room in Wimbledon was designed as a bright and functional home office, creating a peaceful workspace separate from the main house. The building is finished with premium composite cladding, giving it a modern appearance while offering durability and very low maintenance.
A key feature of this project is the large skylight, allowing extra natural light to flood the room throughout the day. Together with the front glazing, the space feels open, bright, and perfect for a productive working environment.
Built using high-performance insulated construction, the garden room provides year-round comfort, making it ideal for working from home, meetings, or creative work.
Functionality
During the day, the space works as a quiet and productive home office, providing a clear separation from the main house. The abundance of natural light from the skylight and glazing creates a comfortable and inspiring workspace.
