Premium outdoor workspace and relaxation area
Project Overview
This bespoke garden room was designed and built by Pecasa Ltd to create a modern, functional space that blends seamlessly with the garden while providing a comfortable year-round environment.
The client wanted a premium outdoor workspace and relaxation area that felt connected to the garden but offered the comfort and insulation of a modern building.
The result is a sleek cedar-clad garden studio with a covered decking area, perfect for working, entertaining, or simply enjoying the garden in all seasons.

Design Approach
The design focuses on clean architectural lines and natural materials.
The warm tones of the cedar cladding contrast beautifully with the black aluminium frames and decking, giving the structure a modern yet timeless appearance.
The covered decking area extends the usable space outdoors, allowing the client to enjoy the garden even during the UK’s unpredictable weather.
