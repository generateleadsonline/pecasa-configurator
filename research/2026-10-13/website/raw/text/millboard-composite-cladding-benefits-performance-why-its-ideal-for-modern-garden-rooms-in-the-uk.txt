Millboard Composite Cladding — Benefits, Performance & Why It’s Ideal for Modern Garden Rooms in the UK
When designing a garden room or living annexes, selecting the right external finish can be considered the most important choice when specifying the external features of the garden room. Homeowners in the UK are increasingly opting for advanced composite materials over traditional wood options, and for a good reason — Millboard composite cladding is quickly becoming one of the top premium composite options.
For Pecasa, cladding is an integral design element that can also create remarkable value through the levels of quality and design that are incorporated. Cladding can also create exceptional value in the areas of long-term durability, maintenance and care, insulation, and overall economic value.
This guide will clarify and simplify the true value of Millboard Cladding, which is the reason for its popularity for contemporary garden rooms.
What Is Millboard Composite Cladding?
Millboard cladding is the ultimate composite material created for the specific purpose of achieving the look of natural timber without the disadvantages of natural timber.
Millboard is different from typical timber boards, as its composition and construction is based on a proprietary resin-mineral composite core, which is engineered for superior performance over timber in all respects, even weathering and wear.
Common attributes of Millboard include:
A deep wood-grain texture
Fade-resistant colouring
Hidden fastening systems for a contemporary and clean visual finish
It is the combination of the visual of timber and the innovative science used in the construction of the material that makes Millboard cladding the most preferred choice for contemporary garden rooms and annex builds in the UK.
Advantages of Millboard Composite Cladding
Remarkable Weather Resistance
Durability is one of the biggest benefits of Millboard composite for the UK climate. Designed to resist rain, UV rays, and extreme weather, Millboard composite will not warp or rot.
Traditional timber will warp, split, and will need treatment. Millboard will remain consistent with minimal treatment.
Easier to Maintain Than Timber
Unlike cedar or softwood cladding, Millboard does not need to be stained or sealed each year.
This provides the following benefits:
No sanding or sealing
Lower maintenance costs
No further work for a consistent appearance
For any homeowner that wants a premium exterior look, this is a large positive.
Modern Consistent Look
Millboard will provide a modern finish to contemporary designs. With hidden fixings and clean cuts, they will provide the most popular lines for the finish of contemporary garden rooms.
This makes the exterior look a part of the building and not an extension.
Why Millboard is Ideal in Garden Rooms
Garden rooms need materials that provide optimum performance and design. Millboard’s lightweight design is perfect for insulated SIP structures.
Advantages include:
Reduced maintenance for all-year outdoor exposure
Stable boards that integrate with insulated wall systems
Premium look that enhances the value of the property
Since garden rooms are typically located near property boundaries, a durable cladding system prevents the need for regular maintenance to keep the cladding looking good.

Is Millboard Energy Efficient?
Cladding does not provide insulation, but does and is critical to the protection of the building envelope.
Modern garden rooms now have Millboard cladding, combined with insulated SIP wall systems, to:
Protect the structure from external weather.
Provide consistent performance on the inside.
Limit maintenance that would be detrimental to the insulation.
This means the garden room will remain efficient and the user will remain comfortable all year round.
Customizable Millboard Cladding Options
Millboard’s variety of cladding options makes it possible to achieve
Vertical batten style cladding
Modern shadow line cladding
Dark contemporary style cladding
Timber finishes that are long life and maintenance free
These options help designers provide bespoke garden rooms that blend with modern and traditional styles of building in the UK.

UK weather durable finishes
Low maintenance living for end users
Modern, aesthetically pleasing, and highly durable materials that provide a high-end finish to the building
When combining high performance SIP garden room construction with high-end cladding, the rooms will provide a beautiful design to the garden and a long life.
Millboard Composite Cladding FAQs
Is Millboard Better Than Timber Cladding?
Millboard combines timber style finishes and a contemporary look with a range of cladding that offers low maintenance living and high weather resistance.
Does Composite Cladding Fade?
No, high quality composite cladding is designed to withstand fading.
Is Millboard suitable for garden rooms?
Absolutely, its commonly specified on modern insulated garden rooms due to the durability and clean lines it offers.
Do composite claddings provide value?
High-quality composite cladding provides a premium finish that enhances the look of the property.
Considering Garden Room Cladding Options?
Good quality cladding are value-adding looks and functions. With a selection of materials that combines longevity, aesthetics, and functionality, the room can be seamlessly integrated into the rest of the house.
A fully customized design allows us to ascertain which design finish balances your property, your way of living and your future aspirations.
