TGO2 GARDEN ROOM
TGO2 GARDEN ROOM
COMPACT & CONTEMPORARY
The TGO2 building has a compact and industrial exterior design which offers the maximum internal size vs external footprint, leaving more of your garden space free for traditional use.
The TGO2 GARDEN ROOM by Pecasa is a refined outdoor living solution designed for homeowners who want to extend their usable space with a modern, high-performance structure. Combining contemporary design with precision engineering, this bespoke garden room delivers both visual appeal and everyday functionality.
At Pecasa, every garden room is built around the principle of tailored living. The TGO2 model reflects this approach through its clean architectural lines, efficient layout, and premium materials. Whether you are planning a dedicated home office, creative workspace, home gym, or multi-purpose retreat, this garden room can be customised to suit your exact lifestyle requirements.
Pecasa specialises in designing and manufacturing fully bespoke garden rooms that integrate seamlessly with the existing property while enhancing long-term value. Their buildings are known for contemporary aesthetics and flexible configurations that adapt to the intended use and garden setting. Built with high-quality insulated construction, the TGO2 is suitable for comfortable year-round use, making it a practical extension of your home rather than just an outdoor structure.
One of the major advantages of choosing Pecasa is the efficient off-site manufacturing process. Much of the structural work is completed in controlled workshop conditions before arriving on site, helping to reduce installation time and minimise disruption to the household. This streamlined method ensures homeowners benefit from both speed and precision without compromising build quality.
The TGO2 GARDEN ROOM is also highly versatile. Clients can personalise glazing options, exterior finishes, internal layouts, and electrical features to create a space that truly reflects their needs. This flexibility makes the model suitable for a wide range of residential applications.
If you are looking to upgrade your garden with a stylish, energy-efficient, and future-ready space, Pecasa’s TGO2 model offers a smart investment that enhances comfort, usability, and overall property appeal.
