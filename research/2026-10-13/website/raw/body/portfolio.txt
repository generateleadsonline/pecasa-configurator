Portfolio Pecasa
Explore the craftsmanship, creativity, and attention to detail that define every Pecasa build. Our Portfolio Pecasa showcases a curated selection of bespoke garden rooms, offices, studios, and annexes designed for homeowners who want to maximise their outdoor space with style and functionality.
Each project in our portfolio reflects Pecasa’s commitment to delivering premium, fully customised garden rooms tailored to individual lifestyles. From contemporary home offices to multi-purpose family studios, our team works closely with clients to transform ideas into beautifully finished spaces. Every structure is designed to feel like a natural extension of the home rather than just an outbuilding, ensuring both visual harmony and practical usability.
What makes our work stand out is the precision behind every build. Pecasa garden rooms are manufactured using high-quality materials and advanced construction methods to ensure durability, energy efficiency, and year-round comfort. Our streamlined process — including significant off-site preparation — helps minimise disruption while maintaining exceptional build standards and fast installation timelines.
Within the Portfolio Pecasa, you will find a wide variety of design styles and use cases, including:
Modern garden offices for remote working
Creative studios and hobby rooms
Multi-functional family spaces
Garden gyms and wellness rooms
Self-contained garden annexes
This diversity demonstrates our ability to adapt each project to the client’s vision, available space, and long-term needs. Whether the goal is productivity, relaxation, or additional living accommodation, Pecasa delivers solutions that combine aesthetics with performance.
We believe every garden room should enhance both lifestyle and property value. That is why our design-led approach focuses on clean finishes, smart layouts, and seamless integration with the surrounding garden environment.
Browse our portfolio for inspiration and see how Pecasa turns underused outdoor areas into exceptional living spaces. When you are ready to start your own project, our expert team is here to guide you from concept through to completion with a smooth, professional experience.
