Bespoke Garden Rooms Radlett – Designed and Built Around Your Home
A garden room can give you extra space without taking over the main house. PECASA designs and installs insulated garden buildings for homeowners in Radlett and across Hertfordshire, with each project planned around the plot, use and preferred finish. If you are comparing Garden Rooms Radlett options, the most important questions are not only how the building looks, but how it is constructed, how it will perform through the seasons and whether the layout genuinely suits your household.
PECASA’s approach combines design, site assessment, local manufacturing and professional installation. A completed room can become an office, gym, studio, family space, games room or flexible multi-use space.
Designed for Radlett Homes and Gardens
Radlett has residential plots ranging from family gardens to larger landscaped sites. A successful garden room should respect the scale of the plot, protect useful outdoor space and sit comfortably alongside the home and landscape.
The Radlett Neighbourhood Plan is a material consideration in planning applications in the area and places importance on design quality, local character and landscaping. Even where a garden building may fall within permitted development rights, it makes sense to design with the site rather than simply choosing a standard box and trying to make it fit.
During design, consider:
the room’s primary and secondary uses;
the best position for daylight and garden views;
privacy from neighbours and the main house;
access for construction and future maintenance;
desk, gym equipment, storage or seating layouts;
electrical, internet, heating and cooling requirements;
whether plumbing or drainage may be needed;
how much garden you want to retain around the building.

Planning Permission in Radlett: What to Check
Householder permitted development rights in England allow certain improvements and outbuildings to proceed without a full planning application when the relevant limits and conditions are met. Those rights are subject to detailed rules and can be affected by the individual property.
Radlett sits within Hertsmere Borough Council, and homeowners should check both national rules and any site-specific local constraints. Listed buildings, conservation areas, planning conditions and withdrawn permitted development rights can change the position. Hertsmere also has Article 4 directions affecting specific categories of properties in Radlett, which is another reason not to assume that every project is automatically permitted development.
Establish the intended use, dimensions and position of the building and check the planning position before construction. Normal incidental household use is treated differently from separate self-contained living accommodation.
What Should Be Included in a Garden Room Quote?
When comparing suppliers, ask for a written specification rather than comparing only starting prices. Important items include foundations, SIP or other structural system, insulation, roofing, external cladding, doors and glazing, internal wall finish, flooring, sockets, lighting, heating or cooling, data connectivity, plumbing if required, access assumptions and waste removal.

Frequently Asked Questions
Do Garden Rooms Radlett projects need planning permission?
Not necessarily. Some garden rooms can be built under permitted development rights if they meet the relevant conditions. However, property-specific restrictions, conservation considerations, listed status, Article 4 directions and the intended use can affect the position. Check national guidance and Hertsmere requirements for the individual site.
Can I use a garden room as a home office?
Yes. A garden office can be designed with insulation, glazing, lighting, sockets, internet connectivity and heating or cooling so it functions as a dedicated workspace separate from household distractions.
Can a garden room include a WC or bathroom?
Potentially, yes, but plumbing changes the technical scope. Water, drainage, ventilation, waterproofing and electrical-safety requirements should be planned from the beginning. Self-contained residential use may also introduce additional planning and Building Regulations considerations.
How quickly can PECASA install a garden room?
PECASA advises that smaller projects can take around 12 working days on site, while a roughly 30m² room may take around 16–18 working days. Site access, complexity, weather, services and specification can affect the final programme.
Does PECASA have experience in Radlett?
Yes. PECASA’s portfolio includes an 83m² bespoke garden room in Radlett designed for family relaxation and entertaining, alongside numerous other projects across Hertfordshire and London.
