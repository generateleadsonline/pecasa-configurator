TGO1 GARDEN ROOM
TGO1 GARDEN ROOM
A MODERN CLASSIC
The TGO1 combines modern styling with traditional garden lodge feel to create what is now a current-day classic.
The TGO1 GARDEN ROOM by Pecasa is a contemporary outdoor living solution designed for homeowners who want to maximise their garden space with style and practicality. Combining modern architecture with high-performance construction, this bespoke garden room offers a flexible environment suitable for work, relaxation, or multi-purpose family use.
At Pecasa, every garden room is created with a strong focus on design precision and long-term durability. The TGO1 model reflects this commitment through its clean lines, efficient layout, and premium build quality. Whether you are planning a private home office, creative studio, or additional lifestyle space, this garden room can be tailored to suit your specific requirements and garden setting.
Pecasa specialises in designing and manufacturing fully bespoke garden rooms that integrate seamlessly with your property while enhancing everyday living. Built using advanced Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), the TGO1 is engineered for excellent thermal performance, helping maintain comfortable temperatures throughout the year. This means the space can be used just like a room inside your home — regardless of season.
One of the standout advantages of choosing Pecasa is the efficient off-site manufacturing process. Much of the structure is prepared in controlled workshop conditions, which significantly reduces on-site build time and disruption. Homeowners benefit from a smoother installation experience while still receiving a high-quality, precision-finished garden room.
The TGO1 GARDEN ROOM is also highly versatile. Clients can customise glazing options, exterior finishes, internal layouts, and electrical configurations to match their lifestyle needs. This flexibility ensures the final structure is not only visually appealing but also highly functional for years to come.
If you are looking to upgrade your outdoor space with a modern, energy-efficient garden room, Pecasa’s TGO1 model offers a smart, future-ready investment that enhances both comfort and property value.
