Meditation & Relaxation Garden Room – Hendon, North London
This bespoke Pecasa garden room in Hendon was designed as a private meditation and relaxation space. The client wanted a quiet place away from the house where they could sit, reflect, and completely unwind.
The building was carefully positioned in a peaceful corner of the garden, the area where the client felt the most calm and connected with nature. This location helps create a real sense of privacy and tranquillity, making it the perfect environment for meditation and relaxation.
The exterior is finished in Millboard Shadow Line cladding in Antique Oak, giving the garden room a warm, natural appearance while offering the durability and low maintenance of high-quality composite materials.
Inside, the space is intentionally simple and uncluttered, allowing the room to be used purely for mindfulness, meditation, and peaceful moments away from daily life.
Project Details
