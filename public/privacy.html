Your design. Your privacy.

This 3D garden room designer is built and hosted for Pecasa by Generate Leads Online (GLO). It helps you design a Pecasa garden room and send it to Pecasa. It is not an order or a written quotation.

What is saved, and where

Your design is saved on your own device as you go, so you can come back to it. When you send a form (your design and price, a callback, a call booking, a showroom visit or the brochure), your first and last name, email, phone number, postcode, any notes, your marketing choice, and your design and its specification are sent to Pecasa through the site's form service, Netlify Forms. If you arrived from a campaign, its source (for example the UTM tags in the link) is included; contact details never go into links or analytics.

How it is used

Pecasa uses your details to reply about your project: to call you, arrange a survey, call or showroom visit, and send you a written quotation. Marketing emails are sent only if you tick the box, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

Who processes it

Pecasa Ltd (company 10714739, Unit 3, 8 Colonial Way, Watford WD24 4PT) receives your enquiry. Netlify hosts the site and stores form submissions; GLO maintains the site on Pecasa's behalf.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you sent, or to stop marketing, email info@pecasa.co.uk or call 020 8146 2200. You can clear the design saved on your device in your browser's site settings.

Analytics

The designer records which steps and buttons are used, to improve it. These events contain design choices, never your name, email, phone or postcode.
