Speedy Construction: Building a garden studio is generally quicker than a full-blown house extension, so you can start enjoying your new space sooner. Think of it as a fast-track path to extra living space. ⏱️

Frequently Asked Questions
Building a garden room is a big decision, especially in an area as distinctive as Hampstead. Here are some FAQs to guide you:
Do I need planning permission for a garden room in Hampstead?
Most garden rooms are under Permitted Development Rights if they meet certain size and height criteria. However, Hampstead has Conservation Areas, so it’s always best to check with local authorities or consult with a professional team like Pecasa to be certain. ❓
How long does it take to build a garden studio?
The timeline varies based on the complexity of your design. Generally, a simple garden studio can be constructed within a few weeks, while more elaborate builds may take a few months. This is still typically shorter than a house extension project. ⏳
What materials are used for insulation and weatherproofing?
Premium garden rooms often use high-grade insulation (e.g., SIP panels) and robust roofing systems. Quality doors and windows with double or triple glazing keep the space cozy and weatherproof year-round. 🔨
Can I install plumbing and electricity in my garden office?
Absolutely. You can have a fully functional space with electricity, heating, and even running water, including a small bathroom or kitchenette. Use certified professionals to connect utilities safely and comply with building regulations. 💡
How big can my garden room be?
There’s no one-size-fits-all answer, but the maximum height is often restricted under permitted development. Many people opt for a large size for a few desks, storage, and even a small lounge area. The dimensions will depend on your garden’s layout and local planning constraints. 📐
Is a garden studio suitable for year-round use?
Yes! With quality insulation, proper heating, and double-glazed windows, you can comfortably use your garden room 365 days a year—no matter the weather. ❄️☀️
How much value does a garden room add to my property?
While it depends on the quality of the build and local market conditions, a well-built garden studio can add noticeable value. It also makes your property attractive to buyers looking for extra, flexible space. 💰
Can I customize the interior design?
At Pecasa, every garden studio should reflect the homeowner’s style and functional needs. Whether you want a minimalist, Scandinavian look or a plush, traditional feel, we can tailor the design to match your preferences. 🎨
