The Process
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms are an affordable fully insulated practical space solution to expand your home and add quality space for year-round use.
Our garden rooms are ideal for many purposes whether it be an office, home gym, home cinema, games rooms or purely a relaxing space!
At Pecasa, we firmly believe that investing on extra space via our Premium Garden Room is investing on your well being, as well as the value of your home !
1- Latest Innovative Screw Foundations
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms come with screw foundations as standard, which are carried out in-house by our specialist team. These are superior to the standard concrete foundations in many ways, including Speed of Installation, Environmentally Friendly and offer improved stability.
some benefits of screw v concrete foundations
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms and Annexes come with screw foundations, as standard. These are considered superior to traditional foundations for several reasons , some which are highlighted below.
Speed of installation: Pecasa Screw foundations can be installed much faster than traditional concrete foundations, which can take several days to cure and are also weather dependent.
Environmentally friendly: Screw foundations require less excavation and concrete than traditional foundations, making them more environmentally friendly.
Improved stability: Screw foundations provide improved stability over traditional concrete foundations because they are anchored into the ground using helical piles, which provide more resistance to movement.
Suitable for any soil type: Screw foundations can be used in a variety of soil types, including sandy, clay, and rocky soils, making them a versatile solution.
Flexibility: Screw foundations can be adjusted to accommodate changes in the soil, providing additional stability and reducing the risk of settling or shifting.
Ease of installation: Screw foundations are easier to install than concrete foundations, especially in challenging terrain or tight spaces.
2- Enviromentally Friendly Structural Insulated Panels (Sips)
Pecasa Premium Garden Room use Sips, which are manufactured and pre-assembled in our local warehouse keeping any mess and disturbance away from your home and this way, the structure can be erected in ….one day! Our Sips also offer great energy efficiency, sustainability and improved indoor air quality.
Some benefits of Structural Insulated Panels (Sips)
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms and Annexes are built with Sip panels, as standard. These are produced off site at our local warehouse.
Energy Efficiency: Pecasa SIPs have a high insulation value, which helps reduce energy costs by reducing heat loss in winter and heat gain in summer.
Strength and Durability: Pecasa SIPs are made of a solid core of insulation sandwiched between two sheets of oriented strand board (OSB), making them strong and resistant to weather, pests, and fire. This makes them more resistant to weather and impacts compared to traditional building materials.
Speed of Construction: Pecasa SIPs are prefabricated in our local warehouse meaning they can be manufactured off-site and then quickly installed on-site, reducing construction time and disruption to the client compared to traditional building methods.
Improved Indoor Air Quality and Comfort : Pecasa SIPs have tight seals around doors and windows, reducing the amount of air infiltration and improving indoor air quality. They also provide a more consistent indoor temperature , reducing the need for frequent heating and cooling adjustments and improving overall comfort.
Sustainability: Pecasa SIPs are made from renewable resources and can be recycled, making them a more environmentally friendly option compared to traditional construction materials.
3- EPDM (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer) rubber roofing
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms come with EDPM roofs, as standard which are expertly installed by our in-house experts, this type of roof offer more durability, weather resistance, low maintenance and are of course, energy efficient.
Some benefits of EPDM roofs
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms and Annexes come with EPDM rubber roofing as standard.
Durability: EPDM roofs are known for their long lifespan, often lasting 20-30 years with proper maintenance.
Weather resistance: EPDM rubber is resistant to extreme weather conditions such as UV rays, heat, cold, and heavy rain.
Quick to install: EPDM roofs can be installed quickly if done so by a professional in the roofing field .
Environmentally friendly: EPDM rubber is a recyclable material and does not harm the environment during production or installation.
Low maintenance: EPDM roofs require little maintenance and are easy to repair if necessary.
Energy-efficient: EPDM rubber roofing helps to reduce energy costs by reflecting the sun’s heat and reducing the amount of air conditioning needed in the garden room.
4- British Manufactured & Maintenance Free Composite Fascia
Pecasa Garden Rooms offer a range of fascia colours that not only provide a beautiful looking Garden Room but also provide durability, no ongoing maintenance and is eco-friendly.
Some benefits of our maintenance free composite fascia
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms offer composite fascia , which is British Manufactured ,as standard . Here are the benefits .
Low maintenance: Composite fascia is made from a combination of materials that are resistant to rot, decay, and insect damage, making it a low-maintenance option that does not require painting, staining, or sealing.
Durability: Composite fascia is highly durable and can withstand exposure to harsh weather conditions, such as high winds and rain, without warping or rotting.
Long-lasting: Composite fascia is designed to last for many years, making it an investment that can save money in the long run.
Aesthetically pleasing: Composite fascia is available in a variety of colors and finishes, making it easy to match the style and appearance of any building.
Eco-friendly: Composite fascia is made from recycled materials, making it an environmentally responsible choice that reduces waste and conserves natural resources.
Cost-effective: Although composite fascia may be more expensive than traditional wooden fascia, it can save money in the long run due to its low-maintenance, long-lasting nature and reduced need for repairs and replacements.
5- Expertly Crafted Internal Tape-and-joint internal finish Walls
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms come with professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish walls as standard. We see a high-quality internal finish as a must—not only for aesthetics, but also for creating smooth, durable, and practical walls that are ready for painting or wallpapering.
Some benefits of Tape-and-joint internal finish Walls
Pecasa Premium Rooms offer professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish walls as standard. Here are some of the benefits!
Smooth, Seamless Finish: Tape-and-joint internal finish creates smooth, seamless surfaces by professionally finishing the gaps, corners, and screw heads between boards.
Ready for Decoration: Once finished, the walls provide a clean, even surface ready for your choice of paint, wallpaper, or other decorative finish.
Durable Internal Finish: Professionally finished joints help create a neat and durable wall surface designed to withstand everyday use.
Improved Appearance: Tape-and-joint internal finish walls provide a clean, contemporary appearance, helping your garden room achieve a high-quality residential finish.
Neat Corners and Joints: Professional jointing provides clean transitions between boards and around corners, helping to create a more refined finish throughout the room.
Easy to Maintain: Smooth, properly finished walls are straightforward to clean, maintain, and redecorate when you want to refresh the room.
Home-from-Home Feel: A professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish helps Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms feel like a natural extension of your main home rather than a basic outdoor building.
6- Designed Internal and External LED lighting
Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms comes with thoughtful designed and bespoke to each project so that the lighting, which is vital to create the right atmosphere , is tailored depending on the usage of the garden room.
Some benefits of Pecasa LED Lighting
Here are some of the key benefits of why Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms come with LED lighting as standard;
Energy efficiency: LED lights use significantly less energy than traditional incandescent lights and can help to reduce energy costs.
Long lifespan: LED lights have a much longer lifespan than incandescent lights, often lasting for up to 50,000 hours, reducing the need for frequent replacements.
Durability: LED lights are much more durable than incandescent lights and are less likely to break or shatter.
Versatility: LED lights are available in a wide range of styles and colors, making them suitable for a variety of applications, including outdoor lighting, task lighting, and mood lighting.
Improved color rendering: LED lights offer a high color rendering index (CRI), which means that they provide a more accurate representation of the true colors of objects and surfaces.
Improved safety: LED lights produce very little heat, reducing the risk of fire, and do not contain hazardous materials, such as mercury, that can be harmful to the environment.
Reduced maintenance: LED lights have a long lifespan, reducing the need for frequent maintenance, and are also durable and less likely to break or need repair
Environmentally friendly: LED lights are more energy-efficient and do not contain hazardous materials, making them an environmentally responsible choice.
