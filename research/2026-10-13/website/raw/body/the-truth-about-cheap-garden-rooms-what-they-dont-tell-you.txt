Cheap vs Cost-Effective: Defining the Terms
To say something is cheap is usually means that there are multiple short cuts being taken. This can be short cuts to the quality of materials, insulation, and even the quality and expertise of the installation.
On the other hand, “cost-effective” is to say that the quality of the materials used are built to be durable and to last.
A garden room is not simply an ornate garden shed, it’s designed to be a real, usable extension to your lifestyle. As a home office, gym or studio, it needs to work in all weather conditions, year-round, and withstand the unpredictable UK climate.
When pricing is suspiciously low, it’s worth asking the question – what is not included to achieve that price?
The Insulation Reality
A major factor in the price of a garden room is the insulation, or lack of it. Many budget garden rooms have:
Minimal wall insulation
Poor or no insulation in the roof and floor
Very thin or cheap materials
All of this leads to spaces that are either too cold to work in during the winter or too hot during the summer.
Pecasa is dedicated to the highest standard of insulation in garden rooms, meaning insulation is included in the walls, roof and flooring. When a garden room is properly insulated it can be transformed to an all year round usable space from a seasonal garden shed.
Insulation is not an option, it is a basic requirement.
