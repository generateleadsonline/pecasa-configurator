## 6. Verified selling points (exact wording and URL)

### Manufacturing and SIPs

- "Designed around your garden, manufactured in our Watford factory and installed by our own team." (https://www.pecasa.co.uk/)
- "We manufacture our own Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs) in Watford, and our core team manages every build." (homepage)

- "Panel thickness is project-specific: PECASA does not publish one universal SIP thickness for every building. As a recent reference point, its July 2026 Bricket Wood project used 120 mm PIR insulation." (https://www.pecasa.co.uk/garden-rooms-london/)
- "We do not sell flat-pack kits or off-the-shelf models. Buildings typically range from 4 m² to 90 m², and our most popular size is around 30 m²." (homepage)

- **Dedicated page:** https://www.pecasa.co.uk/10-year-iwa-insurance-backed-warranty-and-aftercare/ (modified 2026-09-24).
  - Conditions: "Qualifying PECASA garden rooms are protected by a 10-year insurance-backed warranty arranged through IWA, subject to successful registration and the terms, conditions and eligibility requirements of the individual policy."
  - "Does every PECASA project automatically have IWA cover? The 10-year insurance-backed warranty applies to qualifying PECASA projects…"
  - "Are windows and doors covered for 10 years? Do not assume that every product carries the same 10-year term."
  - "PECASA should not be regarded as the insurer…"
  - Possible exclusions listed: "Accidental damage, Misuse, Neglect, Lack of required maintenance, Unauthorised alterations, Work carried out by third parties, Damage caused by external events, Pest or animal damage, Damage to products outside their manufacturer warranty, Normal ageing or weathering, Cosmetic changes…"

- **IWA badges:**
  - An IWA logo is displayed in the stats bar on /garden-rooms-london/ and the 5 Hertfordshire grid pages, linking to the warranty page. Its alt text wrongly reads "Made In Britain".

- **Conflict:** the grid pages say "All our buildings are backed by an independent 10 year insurance-backed warranty for complete peace of mind." This is unqualified, and the brochure's "Ten year warranty" is also unqualified (see CONFLICTS.md).

### Other standard lists that disagree

These conflict with the homepage list and are detailed in CONFLICTS.md.

- **/garden-rooms-london/** and the 5 Hertfordshire pages list:
  - Standard: "Electrical & data (internal fittings)" and "Decoration (painting & lighting)".
  - Upgrades: "Kitchenette & bathroom", "Decking & patio areas", "Smart home features", "Bespoke storage & furniture".
- **/the-pecasa-standard/** ("Luxury is not an upgrade it is our baseline. Explore the 10 premium specifications included in every build."):
  - Includes "Wi-Fi Smart AC Installation – Heating or A/C Included … Choice of Wi-Fi electric heating or Air Conditioning as standard", "Luxury Composite Flooring" and "British-Made Composite Decking … a composite decking area included".
  - Also says "Unlike competitors who charge extra for essentials like foundations, or heating, Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms come fully equipped."
- **/the-process/** says composite decking, composite flooring, and Wi-Fi heating or AC are standard.

**Recommendation for the configurator:** quote only the homepage inclusion and exclusion list, and treat AC, heating and decking as quoted extras. Do not quote any extra price, because none is published.
