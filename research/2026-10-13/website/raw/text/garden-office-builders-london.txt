Garden Office Builders London for Bespoke, Year-Round Workspaces
Pecasa designs and builds bespoke, insulated garden offices across London for year-round home working. Each project is shaped around the site, working layout, access and specification, with SIP construction and ground-screw foundations used within Pecasa’s published premium garden-room approach where suitable.
What a purpose-built garden workspace should achieve
The starting point for choosing a specialist design-and-build company for a dedicated London home workspace is the working day rather than the exterior style. A permanent workspace should provide reliable comfort, useful daylight, dependable power and enough separation from the main house for concentrated calls and focused work. It should also fit the garden without creating unnecessary disruption to access, planting or neighbouring properties.
Pecasa treats the room as a small building rather than a seasonal shed. Structure, foundation, glazing, internal layout, electrics, finish and AC Installation options are considered together. That integrated approach matters because a room can look impressive in photographs yet still perform poorly if the desk faces glare, sockets are badly positioned or the building is difficult to heat and ventilate.
Start with the internal layout
Decide how many people will use the room, how often they will work there and what equipment must fit comfortably. Place desks, chairs, monitors, shelves and any meeting furniture before finalising doors and windows. This protects usable wall space and helps the design team locate sockets, lighting and data provision where they are actually needed.
Video calls introduce another design layer. Think about the direction of natural light, the background behind the chair, privacy from the garden and whether another person needs to take calls at the same time. A flexible layout can also allow the room to support a hobby, gym corner or family use later without compromising the primary office function.
Construction for year-round use
Pecasa uses Structural Insulated Panels as a core part of its premium garden-room construction. SIPs combine structural boards with a rigid insulation core, creating an insulated structural envelope when the system is designed and installed correctly. The company also publishes a build standard including EPDM roofing, Tape-and-joint internal finish, lighting and electrics, with glazing and AC Installation choices tailored to the project.
For daily use, thermal performance depends on the whole envelope rather than one insulation value. Glazing area, orientation, ventilation, floor and roof construction, air leakage and heating or cooling choices all influence comfort. These should be designed as a system, particularly where the room will be occupied for long working days in both winter and summer.
Foundations, access and site survey
Pecasa commonly uses ground screw foundations for suitable garden rooms. Ground screws can reduce excavation and concrete use and, once accepted for the design, allow the structure to proceed without waiting for concrete to cure. The correct foundation still depends on site conditions, loads and structural requirements.
A survey in London should also look beyond the final footprint. The team needs to understand the route from the street to the garden, levels, trees, existing hard landscaping, drainage, boundaries and any known buried services. Restricted access can influence panel sizes, equipment and the order in which work is completed.
Planning and regulatory checks
Many domestic outbuildings at houses in England can fall within permitted development if all relevant limits and conditions are satisfied. Planning Portal guidance covers position, height, boundary proximity, total land coverage, designated land and listed buildings. A building within two metres of a boundary is generally limited to 2.5 metres overall height when relying on the standard outbuilding permitted development rules.
The proposed use matters as well. A quiet office ancillary to the home is different from a separate commercial premises with regular visitors, deliveries or disturbance. Planning permission and Building Regulations are separate questions, and a Lawful Development Certificate can be useful where formal confirmation of planning lawfulness is wanted.
What should be included in the quotation?
Ask for a written specification covering the foundation, structural system, roof, cladding, doors and windows, internal wall finish, flooring, sockets, lighting, heating or cooling, data provision, waste removal and making good. Also identify assumptions about clear access, power supply, ground level and landscaping.
A like-for-like comparison is much more useful than a headline starting price. One supplier may quote an insulated shell while another includes a finished interior and services. The lower number is not necessarily lower cost once everything needed for a working room is added.

A practical decision that matters
Compare suppliers on the complete build specification, installation responsibility and evidence of completed office projects, not on the shell price alone.
Before paying a deposit, the proposal should leave you able to explain what is being built, how it will be supported, what is included, which approvals may be needed and what assumptions the price depends on. Written clarity is both a commercial safeguard and a meaningful trust signal.
Practical checks before you decide
A search for garden office builders London should lead to suppliers that can show relevant London projects, a clear build specification and an accountable installation process. For a garden office London homeowners can use year-round, confirm the working layout, glazing, heating or cooling strategy and complete written specification before ordering. When planning a garden office London project, check access, boundaries, service routes and the route from the street to the final building position before the design is fixed. A well-specified garden office London workspace should be judged on comfort, usable desk space, electrics, privacy and build quality rather than exterior appearance alone.
A final point before you book
Good garden office builders should be able to explain the foundation, structure, insulation, glazing, services and installation responsibility in plain language.
Frequently Asked Questions
Do I normally need planning permission for a garden office in London?
Many garden offices can fall within permitted development when all relevant limits and conditions are met. Height, location, coverage, designated land and intended use still need checking for the individual property.
Can the office be used throughout winter?
Yes, when the building envelope, glazing, ventilation and heating strategy are designed for regular year-round use. Pecasa uses SIP construction and a finished specification intended to create a more home-like working environment.
How long does installation take?
Pecasa currently advertises a two-week-or-less installation guarantee for premium garden rooms. Confirm the programme for the specific site, scope and access conditions.
What should I compare between builders?
Compare foundation, structural system, insulation approach, glazing, internal finish, electrics, exclusions, planning support, completed projects and aftercare rather than headline price alone.
