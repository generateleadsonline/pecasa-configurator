Client Brief
The client wanted a private multifunctional leisure space for exercise, relaxation, snooker and music. A built-in sauna was central to the brief, while his drums needed their own separate room because of the noise they generate.
Challenge
The contrasting uses had to coexist inside one building. In particular, drum practice needed additional acoustic separation from the gym and relaxation areas and from the surroundings.
Pecasa Solution
Pecasa divided the building into a main leisure area and a dedicated drum room. The drum room received an extra layer of soundproofing, creating greater separation for music practice. The remaining space accommodates gym and chill-out use, a snooker table and a built-in sauna.

Result
The completed garden room works as a private leisure suite shaped around the client’s lifestyle. He can train, use the sauna, relax, play snooker or practise the drums, with the noisier activity contained in its own specially treated room.
