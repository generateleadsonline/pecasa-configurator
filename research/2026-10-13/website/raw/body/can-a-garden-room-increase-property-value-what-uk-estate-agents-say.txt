Can a Garden Room Increase Property Value? What UK Estate Agents Say
Value-adding smart renovations and home upgrades are always on the mind of homeowners in the ever-changing UK property market. This raises the question, does a garden room add value to your home?
Due to the rise in remote working and the need for living spaces to have flexible functionality, garden rooms have transitioned from a luxury add-on and have become an integral part of the home. However, what are the experts saying?
Pecasa are specialists in the construction of fully insulated and designed year-round use garden rooms. The rest of this text will help homeowners understand how most property experts will help them gain the most value from their home.
UK Buyers Have Changed
The past few years have seen a massive shift in buyer expectations and demands. Estate agents are saying that buyers of today are looking for:
Home offices
Rooms that serve multiple purposes
Distinctions between employed and living spaces
Renovations and upgrades that are quick and require little disruption to the home
All of the above demands are supplied by garden rooms.
A professionally constructed garden room will add usable space and value to your home without affecting the existing structure. Because of this, a wide range of buyers will find the added space and flexibility to be highly attractive.
Considerations for Property Valuations
When evaluating a property’s worth, estate agents consider diverse aspects, such as:
Usable square footage
Construction quality
Overall condition
Longevity
Current market demand
Potential use
Spatial demand
Most garden structures, for example, do not affect a property’s valuation significantly. However, insulated, professionally built garden rooms (like those made by Pecasa) function as a full lifestyle living space. When rooms are done to a high standard, estate agents will often refer to them as “value-enhancing” rather than “value-adding” as decorative space.
Using a Garden Room as a Value-Enhancer
1. Functional Addition
Most value-adding features are those that introduce new function to the home, as buyers want a home that is adaptable and responsive to the changing needs of the household. A garden room can be:
Home office
Studio space
Gym
Retreat
Because Pecasa offers a high degree of customisation for each garden room, the space can be tailored for real use, and will be considered practical.
2. Winter insulation
Estate agents will often classify garden rooms that are not winterised to be used seasonally as outbuildings or as permanent garden rooms.
Pecasa garden rooms are fully winterised, which means that insulation is found in the walls, ceiling, and floor. This ensures comfort throughout the year and the space is suitable for daily use. When estate agents come across a garden room that can be used in the winter and in the summer, it improves the property’s standing in the market.
3. Great Remote Worker Opportunity
Buyer requirements now involve a hybrid working model. Homes that have a separate, insulated garden office are more desirable than those that require internal compromises.
Buyers view a garden office as:
An asset in productivity
A contribution to mental health
A practical solution for the long term
Estate agents tend to include this feature in property listings because it sets the home apart from the rest.
Will it change the official property valuation?
A property’s value depends on its current market situation, location, and recent sales. A garden room is not going to add a certain percentage to your property value.
Nonetheless, property professionals agree on the following:
Quality garden rooms will increase interest from buyers
The right demand will generate better offer
Unique spaces make homes stand out
The demand interest will positively affect the home price.
Garden Rooms vs Traditional Extensions
Homeowners often question whether a garden room provides the same value as a house extension.
Even though extensions do increase internal floor area, they come with the following:
Higher costs
Structural disruption
Long construction times
Garden rooms provide a more considerate option. Because they are independent structures, they:
Don’t require changes to the existing property
Leave existing internal space untouched
Minimise the disruption caused by the installation
Pecasa is dedicated to the construction of bespoke garden rooms, and each room is designed with durability and functionality in mind—considerations that estate agents hold in high regard.
Design & Aesthetic Value Impact
Value is not just functional. Aesthetic value is just as important. An outbuilding that has not been designed well can lower the value of a garden. On the other hand, a modern garden room that has good proportions can increase it.
Pecasa garden rooms are designed to enhance and complement the property around it. When estate agents photograph properties, a high-quality garden room is likely to become a prominent focal point.
Garden Room Compliance & Buyer Confidence
Buyers show a lack of confidence in structures that are not built to code. A garden room that has been designed with compliance in mind, is a peace of mind for buyers as it likely falls within the Permitted Development guidelines.
Pecasa designs garden rooms to meet UK compliance to mitigate client concerns related to the design not being compliant and the structure being unsafe.
This peace of mind positively impacts buyers’ decision-making.
Market Trends
The following trends will help retain the value of garden rooms:
Evolving blended and remote working arrangements
Need for adaptable living spaces in homes
Increased costs associated with buying larger homes
Demand for ready-for-occupancy homes
Properties with an insulated and functional garden room will fit these trends.
The Direction of Quality and Value
Not all garden rooms will provide the same value to a property. The following will determine the value of a garden room:
Full insulation
Quality Craftsmanship
Lasting materials
Practical design
Pecasa’s insulated bespoke garden rooms are of the highest quality and this equips them to provide lasting value, as opposed to a temporary addition.
While it is true that no home improvement guarantees a return, the following is true for quality garden rooms:
Increased market differentiation
Added value to the property
Increased demand for the property
Better alignment with the desired modern lifestyle
Increased perceived liveable space
Pecasa’s garden rooms, along with interior design, provide a high level of differentiation and desirability to the property.
Summary
The demand for functionality and adaptability in the modern housing market is high. Garden rooms offer both of these in a value-adding addition to the property.
Garden rooms, designed and built to last, consistently provide estate agents with a competitive edge. They offer immediate lifestyle enhancements and, when the time comes, offer opportunities to increase value to a homeowner’s real estate portfolio.
