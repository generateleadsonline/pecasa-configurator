What Is a Garden Annexe? A Complete UK Homeowner’s Guide
Introduction
In the UK, the garden annexe concept is gaining popularity as families look for new, flexible ways to accommodate their loved ones. A new garden annexe is particularly appealing to homeowners who want to maximize their property and avoid the hassle of relocating.
Pecasa, an expert garden room builder in London, North London, Hertfordshire, Watford, and other nearby regions, constructs custom garden structures that provide peace of mind and lasting comfort. This article covers the essentials of garden annexes, including their purpose and their potential benefits for your home.
What is a Garden Annexe?
A garden annexe is an independent living unit located on your property. They also differ from an ordinary garden shed or a summer house as they are suitable for use throughout the entire year and are equipped with:
A separate bedroom
A kitchenette and living room
A bathroom
Heating and insulation
A robust electrical and plumbing system
In conclusion, although a garden annexe is separate from your home, it offers a complete living experience.
The latest insulated design creates garden annexes for year-round comfort. Once complete with insulation, double-glazing and heating, garden annexes can become heated, year-round living spaces.
What Makes Garden Annexes Popular in the UK?
Garden annexes are increasingly being developed in London and the South East for the following reasons:
1. Multi-Generational Living. Many families are now including grandparent or adult child living with them. Having a garden annexe provides a living space away from the main house while being close to the family.
2. Guest Accommodation. It is possible to have and host a guest for a prolonged time with the addition of a private, garden annex.
3. Home Office or Studio. With remote work becoming the norm, having a garden annexe that can serve as a big office or a design studio becomes a plus.
4. Enhanced Property Value. A garden annexe can be added to a home and increase its market value. This addition can be a selling point compared to other properties with a similar value.
For the most suitable room, there is no better than an established company to construct a garden room for you.
Do UK Homeowners Need Planning Permission?
Do UK Homeowners Need Planning Permission? This is one of the most frequent inquiries in the UK homeowners’ concerns.
Generally, garden buildings can be constructed under permitted development rights. However, if the annexe will be used for self-contained living, planning permission may be necessary, depending on the purpose, height, size, and use.
Pecasa works with garden room builders to ensure that your construction will comply with UK building regulations and the local authorities in London, North London, Watford, and Hertfordshire.
