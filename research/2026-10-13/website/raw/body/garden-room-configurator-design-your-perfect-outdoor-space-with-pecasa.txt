The Garden Room Configurator is an interactive, online design tool that enables homeowners to configure a bespoke garden room solution tailored to their specific needs. This configurator allows for perfect flexibility whilst assisting the homeowners understand how various designs can impact on the aesthetics and function of the building as a whole.
