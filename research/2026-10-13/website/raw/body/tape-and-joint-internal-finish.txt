Frequently Asked Questions
What are Tape-and-joint internal finish?
A Tape-and-joint internal finish involves sealing and finishing the gaps, corners, and screw heads between boards to create a smooth, seamless surface. The finished walls provide a clean, professional appearance and are ready for painting, wallpapering, or other decorative finishes.
Why does Pecasa include Tape-and-joint internal finish?
Pecasa includes professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish to ensure every garden room feels like a true extension of the home, providing a clean, seamless, and durable finish compared with basic panel systems.
Can I paint or decorate the Tape-and-joint internal finish walls?
Yes. Once the Tape-and-joint internal finish is complete and dry, the walls are ready for painting, decorating, shelving, and other interior customisation.
Do Tape-and-joint internal finish improve the overall look of the garden room?
Absolutely. A professionally Tape-and-joint internal finish creates a clean, modern appearance that enhances the overall feel and perceived quality of the garden room.
