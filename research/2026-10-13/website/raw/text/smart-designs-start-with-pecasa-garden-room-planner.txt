Smart Designs Start with Pecasa Garden Room Planner
Due to advancements in tech and data it is now possible for a home to go beyond the four walls. Homeowners are making good use of their backyard and outdoor space. At the heart of these outdoor transformations is the garden room planner, a powerful tool in readying your future outdoor room.
Pecasa makes sure your actual planning is smart and has known how to help you find what fits your need in with your life styles and garden room ideas. \
What is a garden room planner?
A garden room planner is a lot more than just an idea for design. This is a strategic tool built to help you see, change and set everything for your garden room before the build.
Instead of a one-size-fits-all design, you’ll receive an especially geared plan to help
Your goals are met,
You use your garden effectively, and
Your garden room harmonizes with the elements already present in the backyard
Your area is equipped with durable components for an extended period
This kind of planning is called Pecasa’s Design Your Building service. It is the perfect model for design and space.
The Key to a Successful Garden Room? Smart Planning
The planning and knowing how to erect something structurally sound is the differentiator between a simple temporary structure, or one that can be permanent and value adding to your home. It is so important to plan a garden room and what goes into its overall success. Without a clear and precise plan, the garden room will be glamorous — if only with respect to beauty — but either lacking in functionality or not achieving what you hoped for it to achieve comfort-wise.
A garden room planner will take these, and many other considerations and questions, to make sure:
Space is maximised
All four dimensions, in conjunction with one another, contribute towards the ideal combination of usability and aesthetic appeal.
Design Acts
There are a lot of details, from insulation to construction material that is designed for all-year use.
Construction Risk is lowered
Because good planning means no, or less, uncertainty. This implies that the construction becomes integrated and all components of the construction are done in time.
Your Investment is protected
A garden room constructed with foresight will therefore allow increased value of property and it will be suited to the lifestyle needs changes of its inhabitants.
Pecasa makes it all come together in one easy-to-follow planning process and balances the aesthetic with the functional.

Fully Insulated Outdoor Living Spaces

Fully Plastered Interior Finishes

What could be a Good Garden Room Planner?
Not every planning system allows flexibility in that specific manner. The ideal garden room planner is the best of all three: imagination, design and function.
Design Freedom
Everything in the garden room needs to be flexible enough to mix and match all elements.
Clear Visualisation
This is in pre-building. The ability to visualize your garden room is essential in establishing comprehension and establishing confidence.
Build Accuracy
The design plans should be consistent in the garden room during the process. This is the objective Pecasa aims for.
Efficiency and Simplicity
A well-structured plan is an essential design element that protects your time, limits stress and facilitates a smooth transition from design into construction.

It’s About You

Outdoor living areas smart-expansion secrets
Garden rooms are a game changer when it comes to assessing structures as outdoor living spaces. For a garden room, lifestyle is important too — you should design your space so that it boosts productivity in your daily life and enhances the value of your home.
Pecasa’s Design Your Building offers imagination the headroom to explore by synthesizing technology, user-oriented design and craft from design professionals. Choose Pecasa and that will be the seamless extension of your home and that will be the space that takes you outdoors. It is so clearly intelligent design that it speaks for itself.
