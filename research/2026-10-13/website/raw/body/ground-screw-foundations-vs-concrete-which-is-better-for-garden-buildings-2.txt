What are Ground Screw Foundations?
Ground screw foundations are a more recent type of construction, big steel screws that are drilled into the ground with special machinery to serve as stable anchors for buildings above them.
Another major advantage of ground screws include the fact that they do not require any excavation or curing. They also cause less disturbance to the land around them, and they are much faster and easier to install. For these reasons, the installation of ground screw foundations for new garden rooms has become a popular choice in recent years.
