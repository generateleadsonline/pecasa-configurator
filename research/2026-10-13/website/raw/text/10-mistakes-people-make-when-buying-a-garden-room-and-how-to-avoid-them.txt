10 Mistakes People Make When Buying a Garden Room (And How to Avoid Them)
For UK homeowners, adding a garden room is a great addition to the house, no matter if it’s a garden office, art studio, gym, or garden retreat, a professionally constructed garden room provides additional flexible living space without the inconvenience of a typical extension.
Nonetheless, there are poor garden room providers. Many homeowners make the mistake of judging the experience from the comfort issues and structural problems to the regrettable poor design decisions after choosing the lowest price professional.
Pecasa is committed to designing and constructing fully insulated garden rooms that are usable all year round. Knowing the common pitfalls of garden room purchasers will assist in ensuring optimal long-lasting benefits, comfort, and operational effectiveness of your garden room.
Here are the most common pitfalls of purchasing a garden room, and how to avoid them.
1. Prioritizing Price Over Quality
Making the decision to go with the lowest priced quote is a common error.
Low priced quotes for garden rooms often:
Have no or poor insulation
Are constructed with poor quality, lightweight materials
Are designed for little use
Collapse or deteriorate in quality quickly
Even though the short term cost is appealing, such structures almost always require additional costs for improvements or repairs in the long term.
Avoiding It
Focus on value rather than price. An example of a garden room that has been professionally built and fully insulated is Pecasa. Pecasa’s garden rooms are designed for use all year round and for long-term durability. Their high level of construction means that the garden rooms can be comfortablly used in winter and the summers will be structurally sound for use for many years to come.
2. Not Insulating to Standards
When it comes to garden rooms most people believe that all buildings are insulated to the same level. This is incorrect. Insulation is the element that really converts a garden building into a proper living space. Without adequate insulation in the:
Walls
Roof
Floor
During the colder months, the room may become unusable.
How to Avoid It
Always check the insulation specification. The Pecasa garden room has been designed to enable year-round, all-season use. This leads to comfort and energy efficiency.
3. Not Planning the Intended Use Correctly
The design of a garden room has to be built around its function. Buying a generic structure without a defined purpose often leads to layout problems later on.
A room for an office space needs sufficient glazing and power points
A room for a Gym will need enough strength and strategic planning of space
A room for a studio gets an advantage if there is a well thought out arrangement of natural light
What to Do
Opt for a bespoke design approach. Pecasa builds custom garden rooms for specific purposes, so it is certain to be critically evaluated from day one instead of being retrofitted afterwards.
4. The Design of the Space
The design of the space is the one that underestimates the importance of planning
A garden room that is poorly designed will
Command a strong presence in the garden
Create unusable space in the garden that is outdoor
Create a space that is isolated from the house
Visually good design is what makes a good garden room
How to Avoid It
Work with a firm that does design work that is specific to the dimensions of your garden. Pecasa ensures that the size and layout along with the placement of the glass, is designed in such a way that it creates a space that is in harmony with the other structures, and that makes it easy for the garden room to compliment the house instead of dominating it.
5. The Planning and Compliance
Most garden rooms can be placed anywhere without the need of obtained permission. Garden rooms are usually built under the rights that develop certain properties. The rights, however, do come with a specific set of limitations in
Height
Placement
Distance from the borders
These can set an occurrence of delays
What to Avoid It
The room should be designed without customs. The Pecasa garden rooms are designed in a precise manner that conforms to the garden of the United Kingdom and prevents the owner from having to deal with the annoying workings.
6. Ignoring Electrical Options
A garden room is designed to provide the function of a fully equipped interior room. Without professional wiring, the room may not be able to accommodate:
Electric lights
Office electronics
Electric heaters
Electric fitness equipment
How to Avoid It
Ensure that electrical work is part of the initial planning. Pecasa garden rooms come with integrated electrical contraptions which ensure daily use in a reliable and safe manner.
7. Not Considering Future Options
A lot of customers place an order and center only around the immediate demand, such as a home office, and fail to look further.
With time, requirements are likely to change. A space with a rigid purpose is a room that will be under-utilized.
How to Avoid It
Flexible use is a structured design for which you will be able to utilize multiple configurations. Pecasa garden rooms are professionally finished and come with insulation, which means that they can be transformed from an office, to a studio, to a gym or a relaxation room.
8. Not Knowing How Installation Will Impact Their Space
Some homeowners are surprised by how construction affects their property. While garden rooms are less disruptive than extensions, a few inconveniences can arise due to lack of planning.
How to Avoid It
A structured process should be your choice. Pecasa has a streamlined construction and installation process which greatly reduces the impact on homeowners and allows the daily routines of the home to continue with as little change as possible.
9. Ignoring Base Preparation
The foundation for a garden room is crucial. Insufficient base preparation can result in:
Structural movement
Problems with moisture
Floors that are not level
How to Avoid It
Groundwork preparation must be included in the scope of work. Pecasa garden rooms are built to last on appropriate and stable bases.
10. Thinking of a Garden Room as a Temporary Addition
A premium garden room is not a temporary structure – it is a long-term investment for your property.
Purchasing a space that is not constructed to last will decrease usability and the value of your property.
How to Avoid It
Consider the garden room a permanent improvement. Pecasa only uses long-lasting materials for their garden rooms to ensure they are designed to provide additional functional space and to enhance property value.
Why It’s Important to Choose the Right Garden Room Company
The builder is often the difference between the successful investment and the costly regret.
Pecasa focuses on:
Made to measure design for garden rooms
Insulated construction for all year use
Quality installation
Finishes that are suitable for the UK weather
Because they focus solely on garden rooms instead of general building work, Pecasa will have complete knowledge in all areas of the building process from design to installation.
The excitement to improve your lifestyle with a new garden room should not be marred by future issues from bad planning and design; cheap cost over quality, and insufficient or no insulation. Ensuring these pitfalls are avoided, will help keep your comfort and value return from the investment as high as possible for the longest time.
With Pecasa, professionally designed and built garden rooms will help mitigate the above risks by providing custom design and insulation, a potential regulatory and building code oversight insulation, and quality installation to ease your comfort and value return concerns.
When the planning, design, construction and insulation are proper and complete to a high quality, your garden room will make for one of the smartest and most functional home improvement upgrades your garden has seen.
