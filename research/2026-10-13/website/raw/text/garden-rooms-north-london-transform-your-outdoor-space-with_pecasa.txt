Garden Rooms North London: Transform Your Outdoor Space with Pecasa
With property prices ever increasing, homeowners are looking for ways to effectively get the most out of their space in a smart way and garden rooms North London have become one of the fasted growing home improvement trends. From a peaceful home office and private gym to the ideal creative studio or added living space, a custom garden room delivers the right combination of style, comfort and functionality.
We are Pecasa, and we create luxury garden rooms that allow every homeowner to maximise their outdoor spaces. Using creative building techniques, contemporary design features and year-round usability, we can offer tailored solutions which give beautiful additions to your home.
The Rise of Garden Rooms in North London
One challenge that North London homeowners often have is needing more space but not wanting the hassle, expense and upheaval of moving house. Traditional extensions tend to be costly and lengthy, with loft conversions only sometimes delivering the required amount of flexibility.
An intelligent alternate is a professionally designed garden room. It is located in your garden and creates a space independent of the rest of the home and suitable for use all year round. Whether you work from home, workout from home, want an area for entertainment or relaxation (and let’s be honest all four are more frequently interchangeable within a week) — a garden room moves with your changing lifestyle needs.
A garden room of good quality can improve the aesthetic appeal of your property whilst providing beneficial additional space and value to the property itself.
Custom-built Garden Rooms to fit around your life.
At Pecasa, every project starts with a conversation about how you plan to use the space. Our garden rooms are custom built for you and your space unlike many of the standard off-the-shelf designs available.
This key driver is the top reason for the increasing demand in garden rooms.

As no single style suits everyone, our team partners with the clientele to create custom layouts, finishes, glazing types, and interior features that match their needs.
Built for Year-Round Comfort
A major benefit of Pecasa is we focus on high quality construction. We build our garden rooms from highly advanced Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), ensuring excellent thermal efficiency and strength. That ensures your garden room stays warm in the winter and a sensible cool place to be in the summer!
Additional premium features include:
High-performance insulation

These specifications enable our garden rooms to become real living spaces, rather than just seasonal garden installations.
Fast and Efficient Installation
Home improvement projects are, seemingly, built for long and disruptive runs. Pecasa takes a different approach.
A lot of the prep work is done off-site too, with precision manufacturing creating many components prior to them arriving at your property. When paired with the latest ground screw foundation systems, this provides a fast installation process. Most projects can be accomplished in under two weeks, meaning that there is very little disruption to your schedule!
And by making this process more streamlined and optional, residential owners find their way into the new space much sooner without sacrificing quality or detail.
Garden Offices and Professional Workspaces
In a world where many professionals are now permanently working from home, the demand for garden offices among North London homeowners has skyrocketed.
Having a dedicated garden office creates distance between work and home life, meaning that you are more productive whilst being less likely to be distracted by things around the house. A dedicated office room presents a professional, comfortable and inspiring environment that working from a spare bedroom or dining table cannot replicate.
Pecasa creates garden workplaces with functional touches — like electric installations, lights, high-speed connection preparation and weatherproofing to ensure a perfect work setting all year long.
