Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
1. Do I Need Planning Permission for a Pecasa Garden Room in Rickmansworth?
Many garden rooms are built under Permitted Development Rights, meaning you may not need formal planning permission. However, larger or more elaborate designs might require approval. On your behalf, Pecasa’s team will handle all the checks with the Three Rivers District Council.
2. How Much Does a Pecasa Garden Studio Cost?
Costs vary depending on size, materials, and custom features, but smaller units might start around £10,000–£15,000. Larger, more luxurious builds—complete with heating, plumbing, and premium finishes—can cost £30,000–£50,000 or more. We provide transparent quotes and tailor our builds to your budget.
3. Can My Garden Office Be Used Year-Round?
Absolutely! Pecasa includes insulation, double-glazing, and energy-efficient heating systems to ensure your garden office is comfortable every season. ❄️
4. How Long Does It Take Pecasa to Build a Garden Room?
While each project differs, many are completed within a few weeks once the groundwork begins. The timeline depends on size, bespoke features, and weather conditions.

6. Is a Pecasa Garden Room Secure?
We prioritize security using robust doors, high-quality locks, and durable construction methods. We can integrate innovative security solutions or external lighting to deter trespassers if needed.
7. Can I Customize the Interior Completely?
From flooring and wall finish to built-in storage, you have the freedom to personalize. We’ll discuss your preferences during the design phase to ensure every detail is covered.
8. What’s the Maintenance Like?
The chosen materials determine maintenance. Timber might need periodic re-staining, while composite cladding is typically low-maintenance. We’ll provide a clear care guide to ensure your Pecasa garden room stays pristine.
9. Can I Install Water and Electricity in My Pecasa Build?
Yes. We often run armored cables for electricity, and the water supply can be managed via a suitable trench and plumbing. Our team ensures everything is compliant with local regulations and safety standards.
10. Is My Garden Too Small for a Studio?
Not necessarily. At Pecasa, we excel in designing compact, functional spaces that maximize every inch. We can integrate space-saving solutions like sliding doors and multifunctional furniture to make a minor plot work beautifully.
