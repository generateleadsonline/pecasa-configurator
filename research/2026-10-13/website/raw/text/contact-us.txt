Contact Us Pecasa
Ready to transform your outdoor space into a beautifully designed garden room? The team at Pecasa is here to help you every step of the way. Whether you are exploring ideas, comparing options, or prepared to begin your project, our specialists are committed to providing clear guidance, honest advice, and a smooth customer experience from start to finish.
When you Contact Us Pecasa, you connect directly with experienced professionals who understand the importance of creating a bespoke space that fits your lifestyle and property. We take the time to listen to your requirements, discuss design possibilities, and recommend practical solutions tailored to your needs and budget. From garden offices and studios to multi-functional annexes, our team is ready to help you plan with confidence.
Our consultation process is simple and customer-focused. Once you reach out, we will discuss your vision, preferred size, intended use, and any specific design preferences. If helpful, you may also arrange a visit to our showroom to experience the quality of our garden rooms firsthand. This allows you to better understand materials, finishes, and build standards before moving forward.
At Pecasa, we pride ourselves on responsive communication and professional support throughout the entire journey — from the first enquiry to final installation and aftercare. Our streamlined process, combined with off-site manufacturing and efficient on-site installation, ensures minimal disruption and faster project completion for homeowners across the UK.
If you are considering adding a premium garden room to your property, now is the perfect time to take the next step. Use the enquiry form, give us a call, or send us an email — whichever is most convenient for you. When you Contact Us Pecasa, you are choosing a team dedicated to quality craftsmanship, transparent service, and delivering a garden room you will enjoy for years to come.
Pecasa is a UK specialist in bespoke garden rooms and garden annexes, serving homeowners across London, Watford, Hertfordshire, and surrounding regions. The company designs, manufactures, and installs fully insulated garden buildings using Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), ground screw foundations, and premium materials as standard
