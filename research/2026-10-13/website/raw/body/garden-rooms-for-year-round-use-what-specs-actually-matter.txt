Garden Rooms for Year-Round Use: What Specs Actually Matter
Simple summerhouses have become garden rooms. UK homeowners want an extension that can be used seasonally or all-year round. The rooms can be used as an at-home office, a studio, a gym, or a place for retreat. Whatever the purpose, there are some technical standards that must be met when a garden room is to be used year-round.
Pecasa designs made to order garden rooms that are insulated for all-season use. What specifications are important for a garden room that will be used all-year round?
This guide is meant to showcase the most important aspects that a garden room must have in order for it to be used in both summer and winter.
1. Structural Build System
In order to have a room that is going to be used all year round, the structural integrity must be safe and sound. The structural system is engineered to provide structural durability, thermal stability, and integrity.
One of the most efficient engineered builds are insulated structural panels which are known to provide the following:
Better Strength
Better Airtightness
Better thermal insulation
Better thermal retention.
And finally, when structural insulation is used, the internal climate will be stable.
Pecasa garden rooms are designed to be used all year round by having insulation in the walls, the roof, and the floor.
2. Wall Insulation Performance
Walls are the most important construction specification.
Walls with little insulation may be fine for summer use but will be inadequate in the winter. Proper insulation will:
Retain heat
Reduce heating needs
Stop condensation
Increase acoustic insulation
When building a permanent garden room, the thickness and quality of the insulation should be a priority.
At Pecasa, fully insulated garden rooms are built, and wall performance is a primary design feature, not an optional one.
3. Roof Insulation & Thermal Retention
As heat rises, insulation of the roof is also critical.
If insulation of the roof is inadequate:
Heat will escape quickly
Internal temperatures will be inconsistent
Energy will not be used efficiently
If a roof is sufficiently insulated, it will be possible to keep the room warm in winter, and cool in the summer.
This will contribute to a reduction of energy costs and an increase in long-term usability of the room.
4. Floor Insulation & Base Preparation
Comfort is also greatly affected by the insulating of the flooring.
Insulated flooring will increase the comfort level by reducing heat loss to the ground. It will also keep the internal temperatures more stable.
When combined with the right base preparation, insulated flooring will help to guarantee the longevity and performance of the structure.
5. Tightness of Construction & Airtightness
Poor airtightness even thick insulation might fail.
The airtightness of construction prevents
Drafts
Leakage of heat
Moisture ingress
Energy loss through Construction
Precision construction as a result of construction design and installation provides a level of construction perfection on joints, seals, and weatherproofing.
Pecasa’s construction techniques result in airtight construction which will provide a guarantee of comfort year-round.
6. Advanced Glazing
Doors and windows are areas to which heat loss is concentrated.
Performance of heat loss control, conduction of light, and control of moisture facilitation is a requirement of glazing.
The design and strategic placement of glazing is instrumental to the control of heat loss and conduction of light.
Due to the bespoke nature of the garden rooms designed by Pecasa, the glazing is set to perform in the best emulative nature.
7. Electrical Planning & Utilization
A garden room designed for year round use should be able to operate as any internal room.
This is achieved through the integration of
Electrical
Lighting
Power
Office equipment
Exercise equipment and creative tools
This infrastructure develops the structure from just a decorative unit to a fully functional, operational area.
Pecasa garden rooms are built to include all operational requirements, ensuring the provision for all construction duals is done.
8. Awareness of Governmental Regulation and Development
Most garden rooms are covered by Permitted Development Rights, however, for year round use, there are compliance requirements.
Legal, zoning, and boundary
Complications with local council and boundary restrictions mean better value for your garden room in the long run.
9. Future-Proofing
A garden room with the potential for long-term use should be adaptable, for instance, the home office can be converted into a:
Studio
Gym
Chill out room
With this in mind, good insulation and structural quality should allow the room to fully realise its potential.
At Pecasa, we also aim for the maximum long-term utility of a garden room, so each room is fully customizable for when its initial purpose is tired.
10. The UK’s Climate is Notorious
With:
Damp weather
Sudden temperature drops
Storms
Your garden room’s structure needs to be considered carefully. So does its insulation. Less maintenance. More longevity.
Why are Specifications More Important than Price?
A garden room’s price tells you nearly all you need to know about its specs.
Budget products tend to offer:
Poorer insulation
A weaker structure
Weaker glass
Slacker installation
Your garden room needs to be insulated to a high standard all year round.
This is why Pecasa does only fully insulated, bespoke garden rooms. Each project is about all season comfort and necessary insulation.
Seasonal Vs Year Round Use
A summer house will do for casual hot weather use.
A garden room for all seasons must offer:
Thermal efficiency
Structural integrity
Comfort
Usability
Professional
The above listed attributes will determine how well the structure functions as desired.
Final Remarks
When investing in a garden room for all seasons, details are crucial.
Your garden room will only be warm, functional, and comfortable year-round if it has great insulation in the walls, roof, and floor, great structure, airtight construction, superior windows, and the right electrical system.
