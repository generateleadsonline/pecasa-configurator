Frequently Asked Questions About Building Garden Rooms or Offices ❓🏠

1. Will I Need Planning Permission for a Garden Room in Finchley?
In many cases, garden rooms can be constructed under Permitted Development rights, provided they adhere to specific height and size constraints and are not used as a separate dwelling. However, planning permission may be necessary if your property is in a conservation area or if you are planning a more extensive build. Pecasa can help you determine the planning requirements and handle any required paperwork.
2. How Long Does It Take to Build a Garden Studio?
The timeline depends on your design’s complexity, the structure’s size, and whether any preparatory groundwork is required. A straightforward garden room can be installed in weeks—much quicker than a traditional house extension. Once the design is finalized, Pecasa will give you a clear timeline.
3. Can I Use a Garden Studio All Year Round?
Absolutely. With proper insulation, double glazing, and heating solutions (like underfloor heating or electric radiators), your Garden Office Finchley can remain cozy in winter and comfortably cool in summer. Many clients report using their garden rooms daily, regardless of the season.

Yes! A garden room is perfect for cardio machines, free weights, and yoga mats. We can include reinforced flooring if you plan to use heavier gym equipment. Good ventilation and airflow are key, and our designs can incorporate large windows or sliding doors for fresh air and robust lighting for evening workouts.
5. How Much Will a Garden Room Add to My Property’s Value?

Garden rooms are relatively low-maintenance, especially when made of high-quality, weather-resistant materials. Yes. Our team at Pecasa can advise on the best ways to route electricity and broadband to your garden room. If you plan on using it as a workspace or hobby area that requires water—such as a small kitchenette or bathroom—plumbing solutions can also be integrated, depending on feasibility.
8. What If I Have a Small Garden?
No worries! A garden studio can be tailored to fit smaller or awkwardly shaped plots of land. Our design team can maximize the available space while ensuring a functional and inviting environment.
