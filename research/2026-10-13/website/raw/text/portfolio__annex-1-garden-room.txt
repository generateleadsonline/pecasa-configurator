ANNEX 1 GARDEN ROOM
The ANNEX 1 GARDEN ROOM by Pecasa represents the perfect balance of compact design, smart space utilisation, and premium craftsmanship. Designed for homeowners who want to maximise usable living space without overwhelming their garden, this annex delivers both practicality and modern aesthetic appeal.
With its compact classic design, Annex 1 is carefully engineered to provide maximum internal space relative to its external footprint. The thoughtful layout makes it an excellent choice for a wide range of uses — from a private home office or guest accommodation to a relaxation studio or multi-purpose garden retreat. The addition of a single deck step and subtle front overhang enhances both accessibility and visual character, giving the structure a clean, contemporary finish.
At Pecasa, every garden annex is built with long-term performance in mind. Our structures are fully insulated and precision manufactured using high-quality materials, ensuring comfortable year-round use in the UK climate. As with all Pecasa builds, the Annex 1 benefits from our streamlined off-site preparation process, which helps minimise on-site disruption and allows for faster, more efficient installation.
One of the key advantages of the Annex 1 model is its versatility. Homeowners can customise internal layouts, finishes, and features to suit their specific lifestyle needs. Whether you require a quiet workspace away from the main house or an elegant additional living area, this garden annex can be tailored to complement your property seamlessly.
Pecasa’s end-to-end service ensures a smooth journey from initial consultation through to design, manufacture, and professional installation. Our experienced team focuses on delivering not just extra space, but a high-quality environment that enhances both daily living and property value.
If you are looking for a smart, space-efficient garden annex solution, the ANNEX 1 GARDEN ROOM offers a reliable, stylish, and future-ready investment for your home.
