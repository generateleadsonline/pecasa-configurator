A Well-Constructed Edition For All Seasons
One major concern for homeowners is whether a garden room can be used comfortably all year round. Pecasa solves the problem with new-age construction methods that have excellent thermal resistance and long-term durability built in.
Therefore the premium garden rooms that they engineer are built using Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs) Offering excellent levels of insulation whilst supporting structural integrity these panels help to improve thermal insulation, keeping places warmer during winters and cooler in summers.
