10-Year IWA Insurance-Backed Warranty and Aftercare
Built With Confidence. Protected for the Long Term.
A bespoke garden room is a significant investment in your home. Choosing the right garden room company should therefore involve more than comparing appearance, specification and price.
At PECASA, our garden rooms are designed and built with long-term performance in mind. Our construction approach incorporates carefully selected building systems and materials, including Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), ground-screw foundations, EPDM roofing, high-performance windows and doors, external cladding, electrical installations and professionally finished interiors.
Qualifying PECASA garden rooms are protected by a 10-year insurance-backed warranty arranged through IWA, subject to successful registration and the terms, conditions and eligibility requirements of the individual policy.
This provides an additional layer of reassurance alongside PECASA’s commitment to quality construction, professional installation and ongoing customer aftercare.
10-Year Insurance-Backed Warranty Arranged Through IWA
For qualifying projects, PECASA arranges a 10-year insurance-backed warranty through IWA, subject to successful registration and the applicable policy terms.
The purpose of an insurance-backed warranty is to provide eligible protection in the circumstances specified within the individual policy issued to the customer.
It is important to distinguish this from a simple verbal promise or an assumption that every component of a garden room automatically carries the same warranty period.
The scope of protection, eligible circumstances, policy period, exclusions and claims procedure applicable to your project are determined by the individual warranty documentation.
Following completion and successful registration, the customer will receive their individual warranty documentation confirming the applicable protection, conditions, exclusions and claims procedure. This documentation is the definitive record of the cover provided.
Customers should read this documentation carefully and retain it with their PECASA project records.
If you have questions specifically relating to the insurance protection supplied through IWA, you should refer to your individual policy documentation and contact IWA where appropriate.
What Does the PECASA Warranty Arrangement Cover?
A bespoke garden room consists of several different types of construction work, materials and specialist products.
For this reason, warranty protection can comprise more than one form of cover.
Depending on the individual PECASA project, this may include:
The 10-year insurance-backed warranty arranged through IWA for the qualifying work identified within the policy
PECASA’s applicable written workmanship or contractual warranty
Manufacturer warranties for qualifying windows and doors
Roofing product warranties
External cladding manufacturer warranties where applicable
Flooring product warranties where applicable
Specialist electrical equipment warranties
Heating or climate-control manufacturer warranties
Other product-specific warranties associated with the selected specification
Not every component necessarily has the same warranty duration.
The documentation issued for your individual project should therefore always be regarded as the authoritative source for determining which products or services are covered and for how long.
Why Our Construction Method Matters
Warranty protection is important, but long-term performance begins with good design, appropriate materials and controlled construction.
PECASA builds garden rooms as substantial, insulated garden buildings rather than treating them as seasonal sheds.
Structural Insulated Panel Construction
PECASA uses Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs) within its garden room construction system.
SIPs combine structural panels with an insulating core to create an engineered building system that can provide structural strength and strong thermal performance.
Using an engineered panel system also allows major building components to be manufactured accurately before installation.
Ground-Screw Foundations
PECASA uses a ground-screw foundation system on suitable projects.
The foundation system forms an essential part of the building because it transfers the loads of the garden room into the ground and provides a stable platform for the structure above.
Every site can have different ground conditions, levels and access requirements, so foundations should always be considered as part of the complete building design.
EPDM Roofing
Our garden rooms use high-performance EPDM roofing as part of the building envelope.
A correctly designed roof, appropriate falls, weatherproof detailing and regular maintenance are all important to the long-term performance of a garden building.
Windows and Doors
Modern glazing and external doors contribute to weather resistance, security, comfort and thermal performance.
Where applicable, windows and doors may also be covered by separate supplier or manufacturer warranties with their own conditions and durations.
Manufacturer Warranties
Certain components within a PECASA garden room may carry separate warranties issued by their original manufacturer or specialist supplier.
These warranties are distinct from the 10-year insurance-backed warranty arranged through IWA.
Depending on the final project specification, separately warranted products may include:
Windows
External doors
Roofing products
Flooring
Electrical equipment
Heating systems
Air-conditioning equipment
Smart-home products
Other specialist equipment
Manufacturer warranty periods and conditions differ between products.
Some manufacturers may also require registration, scheduled servicing, routine maintenance or compliance with particular operating conditions for their warranty to remain valid.
Customers should therefore retain all documentation supplied at completion.
Our Approach to Aftercare
PECASA’s involvement does not simply end when your garden room installation is completed.
A high-quality garden building should continue to perform for many years, and sensible aftercare forms an important part of protecting that investment.
If you have a concern after completion, our team can help establish the appropriate next step.
Depending on the nature of the matter, this may involve:
Advice from PECASA
Review of your original project specification
Inspection where appropriate
Referral to the relevant specialist supplier
Assistance in identifying a manufacturer warranty
Guidance towards the appropriate warranty procedure
Where an issue is covered by a separate manufacturer warranty or an IWA policy, the relevant warranty provider’s terms and procedure will apply.
Looking After Your PECASA Garden Room
All buildings require reasonable maintenance.
Routine care can help preserve finishes, maintain weather resistance and identify small maintenance issues before they develop into more significant problems.
Depending on the materials and equipment installed, homeowners may need to:
Keep roof drainage routes and outlets clear
Prevent excessive vegetation from growing against the building
Maintain external finishes as recommended
Clean and maintain doors and window mechanisms
Check exterior seals and finishes periodically
Maintain decking where applicable
Follow manufacturer instructions for flooring
Service heating or specialist equipment where required
Service air-conditioning systems in accordance with manufacturer recommendations
Avoid making unapproved structural alterations
Natural materials can also change with age.
For example, some timber products weather or change colour when exposed to sunlight and rain. Normal weathering should not automatically be interpreted as a building defect.
Changes to the Garden Room After Completion
Homeowners sometimes decide to modify their building later.
This may involve installing new equipment, altering internal walls, adding plumbing, changing electrical systems, fixing heavy items to structural elements or making changes to the roof, external walls, windows or doors.
Significant alterations can potentially affect existing warranty protection.
We therefore recommend contacting PECASA before another contractor makes structural or significant technical changes to the building.
This allows you to understand whether the proposed work could affect an existing PECASA warranty, IWA policy or manufacturer warranty.
What May Fall Outside Warranty Protection?
No warranty should be interpreted as covering every event that could happen to a building throughout its life.
Depending on the terms applying to the individual warranty, exclusions may include matters such as:
Accidental damage
Misuse
Neglect
Lack of required maintenance
Unauthorised alterations
Work carried out by third parties
Damage caused by external events
Pest or animal damage
Damage to products outside their manufacturer warranty
Normal ageing or weathering
Cosmetic changes that do not constitute a qualifying defect
This list should not be treated as a substitute for the customer’s actual warranty documentation.
The conditions and exclusions stated within the individual policy and applicable manufacturer warranties take precedence.
How to Raise an Aftercare or Warranty Enquiry
If you identify something that concerns you after completion, contact PECASA as soon as reasonably practical.
Providing clear information helps us understand the issue more efficiently.
Please include:
Your name
Property address
PECASA project or quotation reference, where available
Approximate completion date
Description of the issue
When you first noticed it
Photographs or video showing the concern
PECASA can then review the project information and advise on the appropriate next step.
Depending on the nature of the issue, the matter may relate to PECASA, an individual manufacturer or supplier, or the customer’s IWA warranty documentation.
Customers should avoid arranging significant remedial work through an unrelated third party before the relevant party has had a reasonable opportunity to understand the problem, particularly where doing so could affect applicable warranty rights.

Understanding the Different Types of Protection
It is useful for homeowners to distinguish between three different forms of protection that may apply to a garden room.
PECASA’s Contractual or Workmanship Commitments
These relate to the obligations and commitments PECASA gives you under your contract and applicable written warranty terms.
Manufacturer Warranties
These apply to specific products and are provided under the terms established by the relevant manufacturer.
Examples can include windows, doors, roofing products, flooring and specialist mechanical equipment.
IWA Insurance-Backed Warranty
For qualifying projects, PECASA arranges a 10-year insurance-backed warranty through IWA, subject to successful registration and the terms and conditions of the policy issued.
The policy itself specifies the cover provided and the circumstances in which it applies.
Keeping these categories separate makes it easier to understand who should be contacted if a concern occurs.
Frequently Asked Questions
How long is the PECASA garden room warranty?
Qualifying PECASA garden rooms are protected by a 10-year insurance-backed warranty arranged through IWA, subject to successful registration and the terms, conditions and eligibility requirements of the individual policy. Other products incorporated into the garden room may have separate manufacturer warranty periods.
What does insurance-backed warranty mean?
The warranty is arranged through IWA and provides protection in the circumstances described in the customer’s individual policy documentation. The precise cover, claim conditions and exclusions are set out in the policy issued for each qualifying project.
When will I receive my IWA warranty documentation?
Following completion and successful registration, the customer will receive the individual warranty documentation associated with the qualifying project.
Customers should retain this documentation because it confirms the policy period, applicable cover, exclusions, conditions and claims procedure.
Is IWA the same as PECASA?
No.
PECASA designs, manufactures and installs the garden room. The qualifying insurance-backed warranty is arranged through IWA.
PECASA does not describe itself as the insurer and does not replace or amend the terms contained within the customer’s individual IWA policy.
Does every PECASA project automatically have IWA cover?
The 10-year insurance-backed warranty applies to qualifying PECASA projects, subject to successful registration and the applicable eligibility requirements, terms and conditions.
Customers should rely on the individual policy issued for their project as confirmation that cover is in place.
Are windows and doors covered for 10 years?

Windows and doors may be covered by separate manufacturer or supplier warranties. Their duration, scope and conditions will depend on the products installed.
Is the EPDM roof covered?

Is air conditioning covered by the IWA warranty?

Frequently Asked Questions
Does a Garden Annexe need planning permission?
It may. Standard outbuilding permitted-development rights do not extend to separate, self-contained living accommodation. The exact position depends on the proposed use, design, property and local restrictions, so the project should be checked with the relevant local planning authority before construction.
Does an annexe need Building Regulations approval?
Potentially, yes. Buildings containing sleeping accommodation should not be assumed to qualify for exemptions that can apply to some small detached outbuildings. Building Regulations should be assessed against the proposed design and intended use.
Can an annexe have a kitchen and bathroom?
Potentially, yes. These facilities affect plumbing, drainage, ventilation and electrics and may also influence the planning assessment. Kitchen and bathroom requirements should therefore be incorporated from the beginning of the design process.
Can an annexe be designed for an elderly parent?
Yes, subject to the required planning and regulatory approvals. The layout can prioritise privacy, accessible circulation, practical bathroom arrangements, natural light and convenient proximity to the main house.
Can an annexe be used all year round?
A properly specified annexe can be designed for year-round occupation, subject to the necessary permissions and Building Regulations requirements. Insulation, glazing, heating or cooling, ventilation and appropriate services should all be considered according to how the building will be used.
How much does a bespoke annexe cost?
Cost depends on size, layout, glazing, finishes, kitchens or bathrooms, utilities, site access and professional or planning requirements. A site-specific design and detailed specification provide a more useful figure than relying on a generic price per square metre.
