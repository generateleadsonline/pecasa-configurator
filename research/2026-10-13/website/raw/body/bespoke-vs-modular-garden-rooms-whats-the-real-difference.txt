What Is a Modular Garden Room?
A modular garden room is a garden room style that is usually pre built to a specific size and design. For these types of garden rooms, the following is true:
They are constructed in a controlled environment of a factory
They are delivered in complete or sectional pieces
They are designed for easy on site assembly
They are created with pre-determined floor plans
Modular rooms are appealing to most home owners as they have a shorter lead time and the price is usually more rational.
However, these elements that lead to greater efficiency can also mean less room (if any) for customization.
What does a bespoke garden room mean?
A bespoke garden room is created uniquely for your home and lifestyle needs.
Bespoke designs are much more flexible than modular systems in regards to:
Designing a room with your preferred size
Adjustable layouts
Glazing placements
Your choice of finishes
If required, a bespoke design allows you to attach your garden room to your house.
Expert garden room designers create bespoke rooms to seamlessly integrate with the main house, not simply take up some space in the garden.
When searching for garden room designers near me, bespoke design garden rooms offer much more potential.
