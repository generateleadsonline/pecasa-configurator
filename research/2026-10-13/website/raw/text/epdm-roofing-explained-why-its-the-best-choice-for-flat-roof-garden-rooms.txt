EPDM Roofing Explained: Why It’s the Best Choice for Flat Roof Garden Rooms
Garden rooms have evolved into multi-functional, insulated extensions of the home, using modern building methods. Garden rooms provide durability, comfort, and architectural beauty. High performance insulated garden rooms are being built throughout London, North London, Watford, and Hertfordshire.
Most people focus on wall insulation and glazing, but the roofing system is just as important. A poorly designed roof will cause leaks, more heat loss, and serious structural problems.
EPDM roofing has the advantages here.
At Pecasa, one of the specialist garden room companies, the roofing systems chosen for the modern flat roof garden buildings are EDPM. We review roofing systems and look for longevity, ability to weather the storms, and low maintenance and that’s why EDPM are the best choice.
What do the initials EDPM mean?
EPDM is an example of one of the many synthetic rubbers (Ethylene Propylene Diene Monomer). It is widely used to make flat roofing systems.
EPDM is delivered as one continuous sheet and will cover the entire surface of the roof, giving you a waterproof and seamless barrier.
Benefits of EPDM roofing include:
* Remarkable waterproofing
* Excellent UV exposure resistance
* Temperature flexibility
* Longevity
* Low-maintenance
EPDM makes modern garden room designs achieve both the performance and assurance they require.
Why the Type of Roofing is Important for Garden Rooms
Flat-roof garden rooms also have to withstand:
* Heavy rains
* Changes in season and temperature
* UV light
* Frost and snow
* Wind uplift
For consistent and unpredictable weather in London and the surrounding north London areas, a durable roofing system is a necessity. The good news is that a great EPDM roof is guaranteed to last for years without compromising the waterproof quality of the structure.
Great Waterproofing
EPDM roofing has superior waterproofing than all other types of garden rooms. This is one of the main reasons it is the most preferred option.
Because EPDM is installed as one uninterrupted sheet, as opposed to traditional felting that has overlapping sheets. This construction minimizes the amount of areas that stand the risk of water breaches. It also optimizes the following:
* Drainage, as it minimizes the risk of standing water.
* Protection of the entire structure, even when water remains still for a long time.
* The strength of the structure is preserved, even with the presence of heavy rain most of the year.
For a homeowner looking for a reliable garden room near me, the durability of the roof is as important as the insulation of the walls.
Life Span and Longevity
It is no longer news that with first-class installation, EPDM roofing has an excellent life span that often goes into decades.
The room’s precise rubber features allow it to:
Expand and contract with environmental changes
Resist cracking
Withstand environmental elements that break down materials
Remain flexible for a long time
For insulated garden rooms, quality materials that promote longevity are always a plus and also a long-term cost saving option.
Great Garden Room Builders are able to keep the EPDM membrane to give the best insulation to the garden room while also ensuring the garden room fits and seals properly to promote the best insulation.
No Maintenance Needed
Felt roofs can be a hassle. They are usually repaired and/or replaced over time, whereas, with minimal upkeep, the EPDM roofs can last a very long time.
