Modern lifestyles has seen a huge change in the last few years. With remote working, larger families and flexibility needed in living space anyway – more homeowners have opted to spend their capital on good quality outdoor rooms (as an alternative to expensive home extensions or moving).
There are many advantages to having a garden room designed by professionals, including:
Additional usable living space
Increased property value
Separate work and home environments
Flexible multi-purpose functionality
Minimal disruption during installation
Year-round comfort with excellent insulation
Garden rooms are different for the simple reason that they can be constructed more quickly than traditional extensions (which so often get bogged down with drawn-out building projects) and in many cases the new living space can be used within a week or two.

Premium Construction Standards
The most significant advantages Pecasa follows when hired for Garden Rooms Design Herts is their commitment to the highest level of construction quality.
Each garden room is constructed with modern building technology that provides durability, energy efficiency and is designed for the long term.
Their premium specifications include:
Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs)
Pecasa, for example, uses SIP construction that outperforms conventional timber framing with fully engineered and profiled solid core panels that are high performance insulation. You stay warm in the winter and you remain comfortably cool in the summer.
Ground Screw Foundations
Where it works, Pecasa uses a new ground screw system instead of traditional concrete foundations. This keeps your garden practically undisturbed and gives you a great level of building using design.
High-Performance Roofing
The premium EPDM rubber roofing boasts of a very long lifespan and very low maintenance while remaining quite resistant to weather conditions.
Modern Glazing
Maximising natural light, large glazed windows and modern doors lead in to bright welcoming spaces.
Fully Finished Interiors
In contrast to many others competitors, Pecasa has added tasteful and high-quality internal finishes like –
Tape-and-joint internal finish walls
Quality flooring
Integrated lighting
Electrical installations
AC installation options
These features make it a space that feels more like an extension of your home, not simply “a garden building”.
