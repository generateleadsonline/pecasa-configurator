Home Office & Entertainment Garden Room – Hendon, North London
This bespoke Pecasa garden room in Hendon was designed as a multi-functional space combining a home office and entertainment room. The exterior is finished in Millboard Oak Board & Batten cladding, creating a modern architectural look while remaining extremely durable and low maintenance.
Inside, the garden room includes a bespoke built-in bar, making it a perfect place for relaxing and entertaining guests. The interior also features oak cedar cladding, a typical Pecasa signature style, giving the space a warm and natural premium finish.
Built with a high-performance insulated structure, the garden room provides year-round comfort and feels like a natural extension of the home.
Functionality
During the day, the space functions as a productive home office, providing a peaceful separation from the main house. In the evening, it transforms into a comfortable entertainment lounge, perfect for hosting friends and enjoying drinks at the custom bespoke bar.
