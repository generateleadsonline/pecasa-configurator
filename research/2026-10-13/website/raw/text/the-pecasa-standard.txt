The Pecasa Standard – Premium Garden Room Specifications
Luxury is not an upgrade it is our baseline. Explore the 10 premium specifications included in every build.
What We Include as Standard
Unlike competitors who charge extra for essentials like foundations, or heating, Pecasa Premium Garden Rooms come fully equipped. We use British-manufactured materials and in-house specialists to deliver a turnkey solution.
Feature 01
Innovative Ground Screw Foundations
Speed, Stability, & Sustainability.
Replaces messy concrete with advanced galvanised screws installed by our in-house engineers. A laser-level base without destroying your garden.
Rapid Install: No drying time required.
Eco-Friendly: Protects tree roots and drainage.
Solid Base: Stability on uneven gradients.
Feature 02
Environmentally Friendly SIPs
Maximum Efficiency, Minimal Disruption.
High-performance Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs), pre-assembled in our local warehouse. Watertight structure in as little as one day.
Energy Efficient: Superior thermal retention.
Healthy Air: Breathable, damp-free environment.
Zero Mess: Off-site assembly means less noise.
Feature 03
EPDM Rubber Roofing System
Commercial-Grade Weatherproofing.
One-piece Firestone-grade EPDM rubber membrane offering significantly higher durability than traditional felt roofs.
50-Year Lifespan: Resistant to UV and cracking.
Maintenance Free: Zero upkeep required.
Eco-Conscious: Compatible with green roofing.
Feature 04
British-Made Composite Fascias
Maintenance-Free Modern Aesthetics.
Premium composite fascias available in various colours. A sleek finish that withstands British weather without rotting.
No Painting: Never requires treating.
Rot Proof: Impervious to water and insects.
UV Stable: Keeps colour for decades.
Feature 05
Expertly Tape-and-joint internal finish Walls
A Residential "Home-from-Home" Finish.
We use professional Tape-and-joint internal finish to seal and finish the gaps, corners, and creating smooth, seamless walls ready for painting or wallpapering.
Premium Look: Smooth, seamless surfaces ready for your choice of finish.
Added Warmth: Extra thermal insulation.
Fire Safety: Superior resistance vs timber.
Feature 06
Bespoke LED Lighting Design
Atmosphere Tailored to You.
A custom LED lighting plan bespoke to your project, ensuring the right ambiance for work, exercise, or relaxation.
Energy Saving: Low running costs.
Perfect Mood: Dimmable internal options.
Security: Exterior lighting for safety.
Feature 07
Luxury Composite Flooring
Waterproof, Scratch-Proof, & Stylish.
Designed for high traffic and available in multiple colours, creating a durable yet elegant foundation.
100% Waterproof: Ideal for garden entry.
Heavy Duty: Resists scratches from furniture.
Hygienic: Easy to wipe clean.
Feature 08
Wi-Fi Smart AC Installation
Heating or A/C Included.
Use your room 365 days a year. Choice of Wi-Fi electric heating or Air Conditioning as standard.
Remote Access: Control via smartphone.
Summer Cooling: A/C for hot days.
Efficiency: Smart thermostats reduce waste.
Feature 09
Aluminium French or Bi-Fold Doors
High Security & Slim Sightlines.
No cheap PVC. We use British-manufactured Aluminium doors offering superior security and thermal efficiency.
Fortress Security: Multi-point locking systems.
Thermal Breaks: Prevents cold transfer.
Durability: Will not warp in heat.
Feature 10
British-Made Composite Decking
The Perfect Entrance.
Complete the look with a composite decking area included. A safe, seamless transition from garden to room.
Anti-Slip: Engineered grip texture.
Splinter Free: Safe for children and pets.
Long Lasting: No rotting or oiling.
