Premium Construction That Lasts
Pecasa garden rooms promote premium construction standards. All designs are made of Structural Insulated Panels (SIPs)—a space-age construction material offering unparalleled thermal performance and structural strength.
The other important difference is that the SIP building will have full internal insulation – unlike timber buildings which usually are drafty and cold in winter and hot in summer. This means that your garden room can still be functional even in the middle of January and that you will not have to sacrifice comfort.
SIP technology is available in Pecasa as per some of the standard Premium features, which are listed below:

Integrated lighting and electrical installations

AC installation options
These features mean that every garden room can function as a genuine extension of your home and not just an outdoor building.
